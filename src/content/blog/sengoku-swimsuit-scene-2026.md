## Notable Picks

:card[1784] is an Anima healer that brings both :card[1540]'s crit rate per wave and :card[740]'s DMG per wave in a single card, and with 3 target damage amp to boot. This card is extremely strong. If you picked up :card[1672] this isn't a card you need, but it's an extremely strong card in any multi-round format for any Anima player. This card is coming to standard gacha after the event ends, so it may not be critical to MLB it, but it may be worth grabbing a copy to have options. **Extremely strong for Anima — crit rate and team DMG per wave in one card. Not needed if you have :card[1672]. Coming to standard gacha after the event, so MLB isn't urgent, but a copy is worth having for the options.**

:card[1773] is a Divina healer that makes Divina viable in Endless. She's the Divina member of a small family — :card[2373] does the same job for Phantasma and :card[1445] for Anima, all three healers with the same 4% max HP per wave. Where she loses out is how universally useful the rest of the kit is. :card[2373]'s 45% DMG to the two highest-ATK allies and :card[1445]'s 12% less DMG taken both do work in every piece of content — and :card[1445] in particular meets all of a team's defensive needs in a single card. Her 15% skill DMG per wave is narrower than either. That makes sense given those two are gacha cards and she isn't.

Max HP is the least universal stat on offer, too. It isn't really used or required in most content — only in endless, where damage scales out of control. So both halves of her kit point at exactly one mode. That makes her a very one dimensional card, but very strong in that dimension: her progressive skill damage compounds over precisely the long runs her max HP is there to survive. And Divina has no other progressive max HP source — the only one is :card[658], a gacha card that isn't currently available. There are flat Divina max HP options (:card[1540], :card[361], :card[2189]), but a flat boost doesn't scale, and endless runs 100 waves — 15% or 18% is nothing next to what 4% per wave compounds to by the time the mode actually starts killing you.

So if you want to challenge the endless leaderboard as a Divina player she's a must have, and relatively easy to get to boot. She is probably the overall ranking reward. **Must have for Divina endless players — the only progressive max HP in the attribute now that :card[658] is unavailable, paired with progressive skill damage that compounds over the same long runs. One dimensional, but very strong in that dimension, and easy to pick up as a ranking reward.**

## Standard Gacha

These two cards have already passed but get rate up through the event.

:card[1777] is an awkward ranged card that reduces ATK speed on skill, has some additional HP, and silences. There isn't really any specific use for this except maybe PvP. If you picked up :card[1721] or :card[1720] last event you're already set for Divina ranged. Everyone else could look for :card[739], :card[672], :card[1311], or :card[642] in auction, though all of these are less common. **Easy skip — awkward ranged card with ATK speed reduction and silence; no real use outside of maybe PvP. :card[1721] or :card[1720] have you covered; otherwise watch auction for :card[739], :card[672], :card[1311] or :card[642], all of them uncommon.**

:card[1779] is a melee that is a jack of all trades, master of none. Two-target damage amp skill, 30% DR, 45% crit rate, and 15% drop rate increase. None of these are bad things, especially 45% crit rate. Low base crit rate makes this kind of poor compared to say :card[1721], but this is probably usable for farming or as a fill Divina melee. **Easy skip, but not bad — jack of all trades (2-target damage amp, 30% DR, 45% crit rate, 15% drop rate) held back by low base crit rate. Fine for farming or as a fill Divina melee if you find yourself with copies and lack stronger team buffers like :card[733] or :card[724].**

## Event Gacha

:card[1786] is a summer version of :card[1498], and I think this one is mostly a miss. Put the two side by side: :card[1498]'s second ability is 50% Crit Rate, and the summer version trades that away for 45% Skill DMG and Crit DMG. That's the wrong direction. Crit rate is the high value stat, because crit damage needs crit rate to do anything at all — on a melee's 7.5% base crit rate, 45% crit damage is basically useless, since it almost never gets to fire.

:card[341] makes the same trade but does it properly, pairing 45% DMG with 30% Crit Rate so at least some of it converts. And :card[341] still isn't a very strong card, which tells you where that leaves this one.

The Five Swords pairing is the other half of the problem, and it's the bigger half. The condition is easy enough to satisfy on paper, but every way of satisfying it means running a second melee card, and there is no pairing in this set that leaves you with two slots you actually wanted. :card[341] is comfortably the best of the options, and it's still expensive — you're committing two melee slots to it. Any two card pairing here is two weak slots, and this card doesn't earn its first slot for most players, let alone its second.

If what you actually want is an Anima melee, :card[690] is the best support option by a long shot — it isn't available right now, but it's worth knowing where the bar sits. Of what you can get, the original :card[1498] is simply the better card, and :card[1438] is worth a look as well: still greedy, but it brings some defensive support in the same vein as :card[690]. Both turn up in auction from time to time. :card[1539] is due in the September auction, is much tankier than anything else here, and should be very cheap. Past that there isn't a lot in the way of good Anima melee support available right now. **Easy skip — trades :card[1498]'s 50% crit rate for skill and crit damage that a 7.5% base crit rate can't convert. :card[341] makes the same trade better and still isn't strong. Any Five Swords pairing means two melee slots, and there's no pairing here that isn't two weak slots — :card[341] is the best option and still expensive.**

:card[1787] is a bit of a let down compared to :card[1444]. It offers 40% crit damage and a small absorb on damage dealt for sustain. This is a Divina-specific :card[564] with a small sustain boost. With :card[723] in auction this is in most setups a weaker pick. It could see some use but doesn't look on the surface to fit into top comps, as :card[564] would already be useful in that slot. **Not bad, but not required — a Divina-specific :card[564] with a small sustain boost. Weaker than :card[723] in most setups; get :card[723] from auction first.**

:card[1788] is a Phantasma carry with 50% crit rate and a defensive ability that reduces incoming crit damage. She doesn't replace :card[1401] — she lacks his damage amp and crit damage, and makes up for it only in higher base damage, so she'd need outside sources of damage amp to cap her skill uses. The more interesting question is whether she fits :card[725]'s slot for players who don't have :card[725], in a more auto attack friendly niche. It isn't clear she replaces her directly, because a lot of what :card[725] brings to a team is that 60% skill DMG to the two highest-ATK allies, and Titania has nothing like it. Her defensive half also has no way to show its value right now, so she's a card to keep an eye on — future assists or cards could make her an interesting defensive play. **Not required — a Phantasma carry with 50% crit rate and incoming crit DMG reduction. Doesn't dethrone :card[1401] (also coming to auction after the event). Possibly worth a look in :card[725]'s slot if you don't have :card[725], but she can't reproduce that 60% skill DMG buff, so it isn't a clean swap. Worth a mochi copy only if you want to experiment with defensive options.**

## Ranking Rewards

:card[1774] is 8% crit to Divina, which is very bad. Her other ability restores 25% HP to all allies at the start of each wave, which is also real bad. But she unlocks at level 70, so this may be a fairly easy unlock for newer players until you get stronger healers. If you're picking an event target, :card[1773] is the better one — same attribute, same role, and actually worth building toward. **Skip for established players — 8% Divina crit and a 25% per-wave team heal are both very weak, but the level 70 unlock makes her an easy stopgap for newer players. :card[1773] is the better event target for a Divina healer.**

## Conquest Drops

:card[1778] is your standard wild card. Below 50% HP it gets 10% HP every 5 seconds, increases 40% of Crit Rate, and increases 40% of skill DMG. (The in-game description says 7.5% HP, but the card actually applies 10% — see the [known bugs](/blog/known-bugs) page.) This is ok as a filler melee, but doesn't change much from :card[1667], where it trades DR for skill damage. The DR is probably more useful below 50% HP, but these are cards you are only using if you need to fill out your team with a cheap MLB melee. **Filler only — a :card[1667] variant trading DR for skill damage, and the DR is probably the more useful half below 50% HP. Grab it only if you need a cheap MLB melee.**

:card[1782] is 49% DMG on the final wave. This is close to :card[696] on auction, but easy to grab for free. **Great for newer players, skip for everyone else — 49% final-wave DMG, close to :card[696] but free; a strong pickup if your assists aren't filled out yet.**

## August Exchange

:card[1780] is weak but interesting. There might be some meme plays with :card[1666] to have an all melee team with DR and melee damage buffs. It's an exchange card, so it's about what you'd expect. This could be an ok filler option for new Divina players lacking a melee, like :card[1666] was. 3 copies are required to unlock the ability. **About what you'd expect from an exchange card — weak but interesting, with some meme potential alongside :card[1666] for an all-melee team. An ok filler for new Divina players lacking a melee; needs 3 copies to unlock the ability.**

## The Rest

The remaining new cards are all 4★ and lower, and none of them stand out: :card[1770] (12% DMG dealt and 6% max HP), :card[1776] (23% skill DMG), :card[1781] (12% absorb on DMG dealt), :card[1771] (poison immunity and 4% DMG dealt), and the 3★ :card[1783] (4% team DR). **None worth chasing — pick one up only if it falls in your lap.**
