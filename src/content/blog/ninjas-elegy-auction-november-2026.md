We are back on the mid month schedule.  This month is The Ninjas' Elegy, :card[1531] from exchange, and I think we will see Black Cherry Blossom and Demon Blade for rerun.

---

## Notable Picks

:card[1615] is a top tier World Boss card for Divina, and not much else.  When he came out I said that if Divina received a high crit carry he would make an impact in World Boss, and that mostly came true.  Paired with :card[697], he guarantees :card[1721]'s skills crit during :card[1720] or :card[1650] buff windows, which makes for an excellent combo.  Without :card[697] you can lose a lot of damage to bad rng.  That is still playable, but :card[1631] and :card[359] produce comparable scores with less rng, so he is a much clearer winner when you have :card[697].

Outside of World Boss he is not great.  20% to Divina is about half of what a card focused on %damage buffs gives you, and the 25% crit rate to the top 2 only kicks in on the final wave.  In longer fights :card[1494], :card[1540], and :card[1672] are all better choices.  World Boss is an important niche for a lot of players though, so if that's where you want to invest, he's worth investing in.  One caveat: there is speculation the next event will drop an Anima carry, so investing in :card[1615] is investing in staying Divina for World Boss.  I think there's still a modest support gap for Anima, so that may not be a very risky play, it's possible we will see a new meta in a few months if we see good Anima supports.

**Buy for Divina world boss** — a strong pick for any Divina world boss team, and especially strong if you have :card[697].

:card[659] often falls just short of :card[550] but is a good option if you do not have :card[550] where you can throw this on a healer to get increased healing throughput and still get most of the %dmg benefit of a pure support pick such as :card[1531].  This card does fall short of being the best choice, because on your carries you want :card[550] for time extension, :card[1444], or some combo of :card[689] or :card[1734].  All of these cards just offer too much pure stats to be ignored, so :card[659] has to fall into a support slot, where it is a pretty decent pick.  50% ATK speed is a modest self buff, and it only costs you about 5% team damage: his 25% to the whole team against roughly 30% from a pure damage assist.  That isn't a 1:1 comparison, :card[1531]'s 30% only reaches ranged allies while :card[659] hits everyone, but :card[1129] is 35% to all allies in the final wave only, so 30% is a realistic value for a pure damage assist.  If supply is low or cost is high it's probably an easy skip, but this one might be a strong pick if the price is right for players who are still building their teams.

**Buy if the price is right** — 25% DMG to the whole team from a support slot, a good fit for players still building their teams.

:card[1531] is the rare good exchange card.  30% damage to ranged often can still cover 2-3 of your team members making this card close to :card[664] in value where which one is better is very team and situation dependent.  I think this one is notable because as players unlock and seal to sell back these there will probably be a fairly good amount of stock here to build and sell back over time, meaning the cost is probably lower than :card[664] for very similar value on a card that falls short of the best LJS assists for damage like :card[1129] or is even competitive with :card[659] but should be relatively accessible.

**Good pick, build it over time** — 30% DMG to ranged rivals :card[664], seal and sell copies back to keep your total mochi cost low.

The 3 and 4* cards usually aren't really notable but we have an exception that fills several niches.

:card[1618] is a 2 target crit buffer that can be used in low SE teams, and for PvP farming defense teams.  She is comparable to :card[474] who is now pretty hard to find.  Her main downside is in real use her low ATK stat means her throughput is not great, so you will likely want to run a second healer or a higher DR tank card, but her crit buff can really help carry boss floors if you can find enough defense or throughput.  If you look at :card[474] in auction she usually goes for the 4* cap price, so :card[1618] is best picked up while she is in high stock which may weigh down prices.  In the longer term her price is likely to go up as stock dwindles.  She is probably fine to use 4* orbs on.

**Buy while stock is high** — a 2 target crit buffer like :card[474], fine for 4* orbs, and likely to get pricier as stock runs out.

## The Ninjas' Elegy

### Event Gacha

:card[1619] is a melee drop rate booster.  I think this falls more into niche picks, drop rate is nice to have, but it doesn't increase the drop chance by a lot in a lot of cases, if the drop rate was 10% it becomes 12.5% here, that's notable, but not game breaking.  XP boosters are more clearly useful, but he could still find a spot tanking a farming team, especially with a lack of melee XP or drop rate boosters.  That's probably a smaller mark, but if that piques your interest now's your chance.

**Niche** — 25% drop rate on a melee farming tank, nice to have but not game changing.

### Event Cards (permanent)

:card[1607] is a nice change from the (wild) melee cards, which mostly have conditional abilities.  His 25% DR and 10% damage increase are unconditional.  There isn't a lot of team benefit here, but it's also a moderately tanky card.  That might appeal to some players, and if that is you he probably won't be expensive so feel free to unlock a copy until you get better options.  Your other options from auction are :card[1398], :card[1465], and :card[2187].  I think you could make the argument that :card[1607] is the safest fill in of any of those, so a decent filler for newer Divina players.

**Filler for newer Divina players** — unconditional 25% DR and 10% DMG, the safest cheap melee fill in.

:card[1609] is an AoE progressive skill damage card with a modest 25% ATK speed boost to Divina.  There are a lot of Divina cards with 25%, but many of them offer more support, see :card[724], :card[1321], or :card[1528].  So the trade is greedy skill damage vs support on a non-carry slot.  He can work as a wave clearer, and I found :card[2248] fairly useful for farming multi round story events, where :card[1609] offers less CC and lower skill damage but a little team boost to compensate.  The wave clearer niche is plentiful though, so it will come down to auction price and if that's something that you feel would improve your clear rate or clear speed, but I'm guessing it won't be a super expensive card if you do feel it would help your team.

**Buy if cheap and you want a wave clearer** — AoE skill damage plus 25% Divina ATK speed, but support cards offer more.

:card[1611] is a nice addition to phantasma endless teams, 5% speed per round is too low to be relevant in any other format generally but phantasma speed is not common.  She is also an ok single target buffer at MLB, but that's a high price for a niche that isn't great.  :card[514] or :card[741] can still see some play but :card[1611] falls short of the value of either.  Most teams are moving to 2 or more carries making single target buffers a poor match, and with more carries bringing their own crit buffs you are better off running a smaller two target crit buff than a high single target crit buff.  The crit + attack speed combo is fun, but it's not enough to be competitive.

**Skip unless you run Phantasma endless** — the per wave speed is too small elsewhere, and single target crit buffers are outdated.

:card[1616] is a weird card, it's a CC machine, with a silence skill, passive silence on normal attacks and chance to petrify on wave start.  That can be ok for reducing incoming damage, but that's often not something you need, and can be accomplished with a decent tank.  :card[2248] also provides a lot of CC with freezes on normal attacks and has better damage, but the event auction is still a ways off.  :card[1616] has more CC, though I'm not sure how much value some of it adds, like the 50% chance to petrify on wave start.  She could be fairly good in PvP if you can lock down the right cards, but most people just PvP farm so that isn't a very important niche.  At least she shouldn't be expensive if you want some copies.

**Skip** — lots of CC you rarely need, a decent tank does the same job.

## Rerun: Black Cherry Blossom and Demon Blade

:card[658] is a niche Divina endless card that got replaced by :card[1773].  He has a 3 target skill with progressive skill damage, which is fine for wave clearing.  2% might be enough to complete endless with enough layered defenses, but you really want to run 4% and some DR.  You can achieve that by running this card and borrowing one if you can find a friend that has him, but if you want to run Divina endless you should really look to buy :card[1773] when she comes to auction.  You can run both if you want to.  I'm not actually sure if there is an HP cap, but if there is that might be overkill, but you'd get there very fast.

**Skip, wait for :card[1773]** — 2% Divina max HP falls short for endless, :card[1773] gives 4%.
