Happy halloween everyone!  This is one of my favorite times of year, days getting shorter, Christmas lights starting up way way way too early.  I don't think this is especially strong of an auction but there are a few cards worth considering, if the price is right.

---

## Notable Picks

:card[580] is a staple for XP boosting assists.  He is common to find on helpers, and you can run one in your team and use one as a helper which nets a whopping 40% XP.  The upside is you only need 3 copies to unlock and you can sell some back, the downside is he was very expensive on the last re-run, and I would expect that will still be the case now.  If you are a spender and looking to min-max XP grab 1 copy or 3 and sell 2 back.  **Buy if you're min-maxing XP** — 20% XP that stacks with a helper copy, only 3 copies to unlock, but expect a high price.

---

## Welcome to Halloween Tower!

:card[1314] is a decent greedy wave clearer, she provides self skill damage and self crit rate, meaning on her own she will guarantee fast wave clears.  This can be useful in story or conquest, but not something you need, and not for Jewels.  The progressive crit rate is also a bit excessive in phantasma where you can choose from :card[1307], :card[1672] or even :card[1651], with any of these your crit rate can cap very quickly with :card[1314] leaving a lot of the second ability as waste.  **Skip** — a greedy wave clearer whose crit rate is mostly wasted in phantasma.

:card[1310] is a fairly strong phantasma assist, the max HP is kind of nice to have, but 35% damage for phantasma in the final wave is competitive for phantasma teams.  :card[1129] is better giving DR and working for any attribute, but for phantasma players :card[1310] is not far behind, and you can run both.  :card[673] is similar but provides more offensive overall, but a re-run may still be a while off if you don't have it, so :card[1310] is a decent stopgap option.  The main problem is that 5 copies is expensive to unlock a decent but not amazing assist.  But if you want more %DMG assists as a phantasma player this is a good option.  **Buy for phantasma if you want more %DMG assists** — 35% final wave DMG that runs alongside :card[1129], but 5 copies is expensive.

:card[1307] now has a few alternatives that are just better in :card[1672] or :card[1651], but is still a decent stopgap for phantasma players for multiround battles.  10% crit rate to every phantasma team member each wave stacks up fast, and getting your team to crit cap is a huge damage boost in multiround.  A decent buy if you don't have :card[1672] or :card[1651], buy one and uncap with 4* orbs.  **Stopgap** — progressive team crit rate is a big multiround boost, buy one if you don't have :card[1672] or :card[1651].

None of the 4* assists are worth considering here.  **Skip** — :card[1320], :card[1319] and :card[1318].

## Standard Pool

:card[194] is an ok melee support for phantasma players.  Her final wave damage buff also silences your phantasma team, but with :card[1401], :card[1051] and others doing the same that isn't a big deal.  There are better options though, especially :card[738] from the auction.  **Skip** — :card[738] is the better phantasma melee support.

:card[728] used to be the king in endless, and is still a decent option in multi round battles bonded to a carry. But team buffs have increased, and capping in multi round is not all that hard, so this card tends to be a bit overkill, it's mostly relegated to improving clear speed in endless, but it's still a fine option, but at 5 copies it might be too expensive for the value.  **Probably skip** — fine for endless clear speed, but overkill for 5 copies.
