The fourth anniversary events are starting. I got coverage going early with the new card that dropped into the standard gacha, and now that the event data has landed this post covers the full card list.

Going into the event I think most players are expecting a pretty bloody event, and many players who may have sat out recent events may be back, not to mention the 10 full pots everyone got from mail.  The [event rankings](/en/tools/event-rankings) projection has T1 at 35.1M–37.8M.  These are estimates based a standard deviation on either side of the past cutoffs trend line.  If you are trying to figure out a target consider the higher end of these to be minimums and it may well go higher, happy hunting.

## Notable Picks

There are quite a few cards pushing the power level this anniversary, but most of them are pushing it inside a niche — they raise the ceiling for players already committed to an attribute, or for a specific use like Conquest. :card[1650] is the exception. She pushes power for all teams in most situations, and she's a pick for everyone regardless of what you play. If you take one thing away from this post, take her. The rest below are picks for players focused on their attribute or chasing the uses they cover.

:card[1650] is the overall ranking reward, and she is exactly what rumors said, a replacement for :card[549] or well herself.  This is a modest improvement for her card, but I think this one is worth going a bit deeper into.

I've wanted to write about :card[549] for a while because she's kind of an oddity.  On one hand she's insanely over powered for a healer, but that second part holds a lot of weight, her kit is and was a bit awkward.  Crit damage is generally budgeted higher, so the 40% to the team is on par with a 30% DMG by budget, this isn't insane we see it on :card[724] and both :card[2373] and :card[1321] have abilities that come close in power within their attribute.  The thing that kept her relevant long past when her stats and skill had been powercrept was the 15 level bonus.  On its own this is not actually a lot of power, this is similar to 15% DMG, but since it's applied to the base cards it scales with %DMG and it scales as card base values continued to scale keeping her very relevant long past when a card probably should be left behind.  Being the mascot this makes sense, but it also can be frustrating for players who joined after her event being locked out of a powerful card even when, in reality she is not that critical and you can have very competitive teams without her, but players often don't feel that way.

So it's great that :card[1650] is coming in, she fills basically the same niche with revised stats and slightly more power.  That means that she is probably going to be relevant for the next 2 years at least, and very worth pushing for.  Swapping critical damage for % DMG is a solid swap as % DMG is harder to find and more expensive, and the 45% here means she is an upgrade over :card[2373] and :card[1321] even in their respective attributes, these cards no longer come close in power.  Updating her stats and skill also make her a stronger healer and a button more worth pressing in the modern game.  Her 20 levels is only 5 more than :card[549]'s 15, and 5 levels by itself is very minimal, but going back to how level scaling works this is likely to keep her relevant for a long time because it will scale with other cards power as they get added.  **The one pick for everyone — push for a copy** — a modern :card[549] with 45% team DMG and 20 levels, an upgrade on :card[2373] and :card[1321] in their own attributes. Relevant for years and well worth orbing, but expect a bloody ranking fight.

:card[1631] joins the standard pool on Sep 15, she is very interesting.  First her skill is a massive crit rate buff to 3 targets, this is fairly new, there's a large pool of 2 target crit buffers.  :card[1579], :card[359], :card[2185] for example, but most of these only affect 2 targets.  There could be a future where 3 carry teams work and her skill could be useful for enabling that.  Even in 2 carry teams, if you aren't able to crit having a large crit buff is a good way to ensure skills are doing their maximum damage.  She also has a progressive crit damage buff — 15% to all allies at the start of each wave, which stacks across rounds and makes her great in multi round content, and she has a damage reduction/damage amp like :card[661], but where his 30% amp waits for the final wave, hers fires every wave and takes both sides to 35%.  This could make her a good defensive option in teams with less crit.  She should also have the same 70 pity for anniversary making her a decent pickup for a few copies and something to build up over time as she is in the standard gacha, but she is by far one of the strongest standard gacha support options.  Worth adding that even if the 15% crit damage stays attribute locked, that second ability on its own is enough to carry her — all upside for a card you pick up from the standard gacha. **Strongest standard gacha support going — build her up over time** — a 3 target crit rate skill, progressive crit damage, and a 35%/35% every-wave debuff that stands on its own.

:spoiler["The crit damage buff is restricted to Anima allies in the data, despite the description saying all allies. She's Divina herself, so as written it does nothing on a team with no Anima. Likely to be fixed before she arrives."]

:card[1651] is the Phantasma ranged option, and she is really good.  She combines :card[1307] and :card[740] in a single card, and she has a solid skill to boot.  Lack of self crit damage keeps her from being your carry of choice in World Boss, but she is unbeatable as a carry in multi round content.  We had :card[1731] which was also useful as a :card[740] replacement in endless and multi round, for the most part :card[1651] is just better.  Her skill is single target, but it's massive.  It's possible to skip if you have :card[1672] for progressive crit rate, but :card[1651] is better for phantasma teams, but depending on which other cards you want to target here you might need to pick and choose.  On copies, her crit rate lands at 4 and the Phantasma damage at MLB, so the full package is a real investment. You don't need to get there for her to be worth playing — she's a carry with a massive skill from the first copy — but if you want the strongest possible multi round Phantasma team there isn't really a choice here, she's pushing the power level. **Best in slot for multi round Phantasma — grab the discounted mochi copy** — :card[1307] and :card[740] in one card with a massive single target skill. Worth playing from the first copy.

:card[2250] is an Anima melee leader, he offers 25% crit rate to Anima team members as leader, and 20% skill damage to Anima team members at the start of each wave.  He is very comparable to :card[734] who is while not meta defining still a very solid Phantasma lead.  That 20% is 5% more than :card[734]'s 15%.  Anima has had leads before — :card[732] gives 25% Anima damage — but crit rate is the more valuable stat, and that's what makes this one worth looking at rather than another damage lead. The catch is that the Anima lead slot is harder to fill than it looks, because :card[1650] leads any attribute and will want that seat on most teams. That pushes him toward the niche uses, and that is where he is good. Many players use :card[734] in Conquest because he can wave clear, has good stats, is tanky enough, and gives big buffs to a carry behind him.  Anima doesn't have any recent solo carries to borrow, but that could easily change and make this a very solid strat.  Probably not something you should stretch for if you aren't focused on Anima, but a fairly safe buy given the longevity of :card[734]. :card[413] serves the same niche but being very hold only has 14%, making :card[2250] a solid crit rate option for Anima.  **Very solid for Anima focused players, skippable otherwise** — 25% Anima crit rate as leader and 20% skill damage, ahead of :card[413]'s 14%. :card[1650] wants the lead seat on most teams, so this leans niche and Conquest, but it's worth 140 mochi.

## Standard Gacha

:card[1631] also joins the standard pool this month, and she's covered up in Notable Picks.

:card[1051] joins the standard gacha pool, and she's fairly solid as a support ranged. She offers 50% skill damage to the top 2 ATK cards, as well as an Andromeda-esque last-round 35% damage with a 5 second silence.

We discussed :card[1401] and :card[1721] in the last two posts. A key differentiator for :card[1721] now is that with higher skill damage from additional levels and base skill damage, it's easier to cap skill damage. :card[1401] often relies on :card[725] for 60% skill damage and defense in the form of an ATK slow on the boss. :card[1051] provides similar support but trades that defense for more offense — so long as you aren't also relying on :card[725] for skill damage. If you're able to live, that could be a solid trade, and it's worth a look if you're a newer player building toward a :card[1401] team.

I don't think she's meta defining, but she should be in the discussion for Phantasma reserves and support, especially for players who aren't hitting the skill damage cap. She probably falls below strong Phantasma supports like :card[734], :card[2373], or :card[1669], and some that aren't locked to a specific type like :card[549], :card[1720], :card[739], or :card[514] — but she isn't far behind, so she's probably a big improvement for a lot of players.

As to whether you should pull: given that she's in the standard gacha and discounted, she's a good pickup for Phantasma players who need stronger reserves and aren't hitting the skill damage cap. I wouldn't invest more than one copy unless you're a big spender, though — there's a good chance we see some more solid anniversary cards, and you can potentially pick up additional copies while pulling any banner for the event, or via the standard gacha after it ends. More importantly, try to hold at least 70 invokers going into the event — that's a full pity, so it's the difference between being able to guarantee a card you actually want and hoping for it. If pulling her would drop you below that, skip her. **Solid Phantasma support, not meta defining — a cheap pickup for players below the skill damage cap, but one copy is plenty.**

## Event Cards

The story drops five cards for free, no ranking required — clear the floor and they're yours.

:card[2236] is a Phantasma tank that doesn't quite rise to the level of :card[738] who we have covered in depth, but she is insanely tanky.  40% has been mostly reserved for conditionals with the exception of :card[1539] and the 4 star :card[670].  This can be very helpful to newer players where tankiness still matters in clearing content, more senior players probably want to transition to more support oriented tanks so you can blow through floors fast enough tankiness does not matter.  **Free and very tanky, best for newer players** — 40% DR plus lifesteal; senior players will want to move to support oriented tanks instead.

:card[2248] is the floor 80 drop, pretty similar to his Xmas version :card[1435] but pushing the progressive skill damage up to 20%.  That's a notable increase in longer content, so he could be useful in endless or other multi round content, so he isn't a card you need. **Easy free pickup, not a card you need** — 20% progressive skill damage, a step up on :card[1435] in endless and multi round.

## Ranking Rewards

:card[1650] is the overall ranking reward and the headline card of the event — she's covered up in Notable Picks.

:card[2245] is the half ranking and is an ok filler for newer players.  Her self damage increase is ok in endless and multi wave, and ATK speed is not that common in Anima so she's got a minor team buff, but lack of major team buffs will probably keep her out of most lineups, but she could be useful for newer players looking for progressive auto attackers with multi target skill damage. **Filler for newer players** — a self damage boost and Anima ATK speed, but the lack of a major team buff keeps her out of most lineups.

## Event Gacha

Each 5★ gets a short solo window at pity 70, and all three are also on a 140 mochi exchange Sep 6–15 at limit 1 each. :card[1651] and :card[2250] are covered up in Notable Picks.

:card[1129] is a solid defensive assist option.  We've seen a number of 35% damage to the team in final round assists, and instead of going with some kind of double offense she applies a 15% damage reduction to your team.  With the increase in :card[1650] stats and skill heal, it may be that this ends up being enough defense for her to keep a team alive in World Boss.  35% isn't crazy being we have :card[673], :card[1734], around this same mark, but more availability of team wide buffs also could potentially open up 3 carry options in the future, but probably not yet.  I suspect this isn't a card you need, but it's certainly a good defensive option, and discounted a solid pickup for 140 mochi. **Good defensive option, not required** — 35% final wave team DMG plus 15% DR, and cheap at 140 mochi.

## September Exchange

:card[2253] is the monthly exchange, this one is actually quite good for new players.  Her 20% crit rate falls short of :card[2250] but it only costs a single copy to unlock, and 20% crit rate is huge for new players.  **Cheap and strong for new players** — 20% Anima crit rate off a single copy.

## The Rest

:card[2240], :card[2238] and :card[2252] are on the cheap 4★ banner Aug 28 – Sep 6 at pity 25, and on a 35 mochi exchange Sep 10–13 at limit 5. :card[2244] and :card[2242] are event only.

:card[2238] grants 23% crit rate and powercreeps :card[1319], probably worth picking up for 3 copies at 35 mochi each.  Crit rate is expensive, and there could be niche uses, but in most cases :card[1444] is much stronger, for newer players without strong assists these could be a good way to boost targeted crit rate, and could combo well as a starter with :card[2253]. **Worth 3 copies at 35 mochi for newer players** — 23% crit rate, powercreeps :card[1319], but :card[1444] is much stronger.

:card[2244] has a large 7% HP per 5, this could be very helpful for newer players who are struggling to keep a tank alive.  It's also a tower drop so an easy pickup and there aren't many assists for this stat, although it's very niche. **Niche, but a free tower pickup** — not many assists offer this stat.

The other cards are all in the bucket of grab a copy if it's easy but not worth pushing for except as placeholders. **None worth chasing** — placeholders only.
