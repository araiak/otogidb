## Notable Picks

Lets get the good stuff up front here.  Anyone who missed the original run, or couldn't put in enough hours in the mochi mines to afford invokers, has been waiting for this auction.

### Limited Jewel Summons

:card[1401] is the best phantasma ranged carry available, the best multiround carry, and easily one of the best two in the game for all content at time of writing.  Her main downside is a 5s silence at the start of the last wave, this is barely an annoyance for a ranged carry with damage amp on skill, 25% DMG to team members, and self 30% crit rate and 60% crit damage.  If you play phantasma this is a card you want to have.  If you don't, she still stands on her own, because that 30% crit rate and 60% crit damage are Self and follow her onto any team, and she's Phantasma herself so the 25% DMG lands on her regardless.  Crit rate is the stat you can't cheaply buy — it runs about three times the ability budget of flat DMG% per point, and the reason crit damage is so cheap is that it does nothing until you have the rate to land it.  So a carry bringing her own 30% is bringing the half of the crit engine that's actually scarce, and hanging :card[1444] on her is where the two halves meet.  :card[1721] isn't in this auction but he's who :card[1401] gets compared to, and the split is worth knowing: most of his kit only switches on in the final round, which is free in world boss because that's a single round, and dead weight in a five wave fight where her crit rate has been working since wave one.  Short fights are his, long fights are hers.  And a lot of why he's the world boss pick right now is the support standing behind him, :card[1578] being the clearest example — 45% DMG to the two highest ATK Divina allies, and 10% more battle time on top, which in a damage race is most of another 10% by itself.  That's a snapshot, not a verdict, and one strong enough phantasma support tips it straight back to her.  **Top pick for Phantasma and a genuinely strong carry outside it — she brings the half of the crit engine you can't buy cheap.**

:card[1444] is one of the strongest assists currently available.  75% skill damage and 75% crit damage brought the game to a new power level after several cards like :card[645].  That's the appeal of a greedy assist: instead of a little for everyone, the whole budget lands on one card, so your carry runs way out ahead of the rest of the team on damage and still picks up every team wide buff you're running on top of that.  None of it is attribute locked either.  For most players she's the card to buy out of this auction.  There's exactly one situation where you'd look past 75/75, and you can check it before you spend anything: borrow a friend's helper who already has her bonded and check that helper's skill damage.  Skills cap at 999,999.  Anything landing under the cap and you're fine, the 75% is doing its job.  Everything pinned at 999,999 and you have skill damage you can't spend, which means there's potentially room to move some of those stats somewhere they'd still count.  That's where :card[689] and :card[1734] come in, and they're worth knowing about less as options today than as a sign of where the pressure comes from.  Both are attribute gated where she isn't, :card[689] to Divina and :card[1734] to Phantasma, and neither reaches its headline number off your copy alone — half of it comes from a team wide ability that only stacks if a friend is running the same card.  Both also only turn up in the final wave, the one wave :card[1444] arrives at having already carried the rest of the fight.  So it's a final wave niche rather than a multiround one, it only opens up once part of your 75% is spilling over the cap, and neither card is buyable right now anyway.  Some players are experimenting there and whether it's working I couldn't tell you.  So the thesis is simple enough: she's the best in most situations, the competition only gets real as power creep pushes more and more teams into the skill damage cap, and until that happens she's a fairly safe buy.  **Top pick — arguably the best assist in the game, and the only one of the three that doesn't care what you play.**

## Event Cards

:card[1398] is not very notable, it should be cheap if you need Divina melee filler.  There are a ton of these new wild melee cards, and this one is fine.  This one is better than :card[2187] but we will later get :card[1607] which is unconditional.  Not a lot to say here.

:card[1399] is the half ranking card.  This one still is not great but is on par with some of the disappointing half rankings we have had of late.  Probably not worth mochi.  If you need a divina ranged there are better places to put it, :card[1311] is the strongest option on auction recently, :card[1402] is sitting in this same auction, and :card[1435] should be cheaper if you just want a body in the slot.

:card[1402] I want to revisit.  I called her a head scratcher when the ranking was announced and she's a little better than I gave her credit for — though mostly for lack of other defensive options, and because that defense is actually relevant in endless.  She is probably the best of the progressive skill multi target cards, and the reason is that most of them fail to offer anything beyond the first ability.  Her second is a true improvement, the 50% ATK speed reduction on the highest ATK enemy, and that's what separates her from the pack.  She probably could be used in multiround content where you want to clear waves more quickly, or as a stopgap if you need to reduce incoming damage over time.  The honest caveat is that this niche isn't vital in any content — nothing is gated behind it.  She probably won't be expensive for an overall, but stock is still low, which means she may have questionable value.  Still, this is one of the better ways to reduce damage for mochi.

The rest of the batch — :card[1389], :card[1390], :card[1391], :card[1403] and :card[1400] — are all 3 and 4 star fillers and none of them are strong.  Nothing here is worth much consideration, grab them if you're completing a collection and otherwise don't think about it.

### Limited Jewel Summons

:card[1404] is a gacha melee card.  She has 15% Skill Damage to all divina team members, and reduces Crit Damage by all foes in the final wave.  This isn't terrible but not great either, at least :card[1404] provides some support which makes her a decent option for a filler if you don't have better divina melee options.  But this card isn't worth spending jewels on.  The best divina melee options are :card[724] and :card[733], but neither is available right now, so realistically any mochi filler — :card[1780], :card[1607], :card[2187] — is a better use of resources than spending real money on a weak support melee.

## Exchange Card

:card[1465] is another divina melee, she has a low damage skill with increase in skill damage, easy skip.  The current exchange :card[1780] while not great is a better option, her skill hits all targets and out damages :card[1465] once it's levelled up, and she boosts melee damage including her own.  If you are looking for a melee divina :card[1398] should be dirt cheap and has the best skill damage and most tanky of the bunch, and :card[1607] is another option coming later, though he's mediocre in a sea of mediocre melee and not something to wait around for.

## Guild Conquest

:card[715] is returning to guild conquest and got missed in the first pass.  She's 28% DMG to all ranged allies, an OK assist that lands at the bottom of a long list of usable assists.  :card[1531] does the same job for 30% to the same set and outclasses her, though the two do stack if you want both.  :card[664], our staple %DMG assist from auction, is slightly better than either — mostly because of the cliff at 2 targets.  The third target doesn't gain much, so even minor gains to your top 2 ATK will generally do more damage.  There is some future upside for :card[715] and :card[1531] if 3 carry teams ever start getting real support, but that isn't likely to materialize soon and both may be powercrept by then.  Buy it if the price is right and you don't have better assists, or if you need some flexibility in your assists and can't wait for :card[1531].

## Rerun

The second re-run is probably Two Santas, but you have seen these cards more recently in special auction.

:card[613] is a progressive skill carry with some self crit rate and crit damage.  :card[1401] is in all ways the better card, and supply of :card[613] may be fairly low so I'm not sure what the pricing looks like.  If you have jewels try to buy :card[1401], leave :card[613] supply for the collectors.

:card[614] is a 10% EXP boost, this is one of the weaker EXP boost assists, but its an EXP boost assist.  What she has going for her is that the competition is hard to actually get hold of right now: :card[580] isn't coming back for some time, :card[751] is probably still a while off on a second rerun, and :card[708] is auction-and-mochi but a bit rare and pricey, often in the 400 range, and needs 4 copies to unlock — though you can sell 3 back afterward.  So call it a plus for lack of alternatives.  It doesn't change that her value is very low compared to the other assist EXP boosters.  The main problem here is its a bit unlikely to be a justifiable cost.  These second re-run cards stock is often fairly low so cost may be a bit high.  For this one honestly one copy is probably fine if you need to get it.  You might if you are trying to absolutely max EXP boosts, but it could be a bit expensive.  In any case its almost certainly not worth 4 copies for 5 levels, that is a nice little damage boost but its a little damage boost, and its very expensive.  Grab 1 if you need the EXP boost, otherwise skip.  One thing worth flagging: the EXP boost ability is stackable, so unlike a lot of assists this isn't a one-and-done buff — running her as an assist on multiple cards stacks the 10%.  Still low value on its own, but it's a fallback worth knowing about if you end up with multiple copies, or later decide the +5 level ability is worth unlocking with 4.

## TL;DR

Limited Jewel Summons:
- :card[1444] — **Top pick** — 75/75 on any carry, no attribute lock; only look at :card[689] or :card[1734] if every skill you cast already hits 999,999, and neither is buyable right now anyway
- :card[1401] — **Top pick for Phantasma, strong anywhere** — best multiround carry, and her crit rate is the half of the crit engine you can't buy cheap; pairs with :card[1444]
- :card[1404] — **Not worth jewels** — :card[724] and :card[733] are the cards you actually want and neither is available, so a mochi filler beats spending money on a weak support melee

Event Cards:
- :card[1398] — **Cheap filler** — fine if you need Divina melee, better than :card[2187], but :card[1607] is unconditional and comes later
- :card[1399] — **Skip** — probably not worth mochi; :card[1311] is the strongest divina ranged, :card[1402] is in this same auction, :card[1435] is the cheap body
- :card[1402] — **Better than I first called her** — the best of the progressive skill multi target cards because her second ability is a true improvement, and the defense is relevant in endless. The niche isn't vital in any content, but she's one of the better ways to reduce damage for mochi
- :card[1389], :card[1390], :card[1391], :card[1403], :card[1400] — **Skip** — 3 and 4 star fillers, collection only

Exchange:
- :card[1465] — **Easy skip** — :card[1780] on the current exchange is the better card, :card[1398] should be dirt cheap, and :card[1607] is another option later but not worth waiting for

Guild Conquest:
- :card[715] — **Buy if the price is right** — 28% DMG to all ranged, but outclassed by :card[1531] at 30% (they do stack) and by :card[664], which wins on the 2 target cliff. Upside only if 3 carry teams ever get real support

Rerun — Two Santas:
- :card[613] — **Skip** — :card[1401] is better in all ways; leave the supply for the collectors
- :card[614] — **Grab 1 if you want the EXP boost** — very low value next to the other EXP assists, but :card[580], :card[751] and :card[708] are all hard to get right now. Almost certainly not worth 4 copies for 5 levels. Her EXP boost ability is stackable across multiple assist slots — low value, but a fallback if you unlock the level boost or end up with multiple copies.
