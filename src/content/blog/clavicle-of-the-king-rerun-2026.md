Clavicle of the King won the poll and is back as a one-time re-run, filling the gap before the next event which has been pushed to Oct 18.  This probably isn't exciting for players who have been chasing the meta as most of these cards have been powercrept or have been on auction just recently.  For new players this is a great opportunity to hop in, grab a few cards just below the top end that can probably make all but the best teams a bit stronger. The event cards :card[738] and :card[739] are the real story here: :card[739] is usually out of stock and :card[738] is down to a couple of copies, so this is a good restock for both.

The 3rd anniversary gacha cards are on a second pass too, but nothing about them has changed since I covered them in the [Anniversary Special Auction](/blog/anniversary-auction-september-2026) a few weeks ago, so I'm not going to rehash them in full. The big difference this time is they're on a mochi exchange instead of cash, which changes the value of a few of them, so the TL;DR re-rates them for their mochi prices, generally this is probably one to save your invokers but a few cards are great buys for mochi, especially :card[734].

---

## Event Cards

:card[739] is a ranking reward. She has the exact same skill as :card[1720] — a DMG boost to the 2 highest ATK allies — and the difference is in the abilities. :card[1720] gives a flat 50% crit DMG to your top two, but that's a level 90 ability, so it wants a full set of copies and :card[1721] on the team. :card[739] gives 25% crit DMG to the same top two at the start of every wave, live at level 71, so 2 copies. That means she matches :card[1720] from wave 2 and passes her after that, where :card[1720] is the stronger pick in short fights like world boss. She's been surpassed as the go-to damage buffer, but in multi-wave content she still holds her own, and she works as a reserve for any attribute since the crit DMG isn't attribute locked. Her 45% skill DMG leader ability is level 90, so don't plan on her as a lead unless you're getting 5 copies, and Divina is light in damage skills on strong cards so it's often hard to get full value out of. For newer players she's a great reserve pick: 2 copies turns on a crit DMG buff that works for any attribute and keeps growing every wave, which is exactly what you want in multi-round content like conquest and special story. **Aim for at least 2 copies, a great reserve for new players** — especially in multi-round battles.

:card[738] is the story card, unlocked after chapter 4, and he's been a staple for F2P Phantasma players, I think he may have more card mentions in blogs than any other card. His 25% crit DMG to 2 random Phantasma allies every wave is live on a single copy, and the 20% Phantasma team DMG on the final wave needs 3. The random targeting is why he's at his best in multi-wave content: every wave rolls again, so over 5 waves the stacks have time to land on the cards you actually want buffed. Put him next to :card[739] and it's clear there are better support abilities out there, since hers always hits your top two, but for a melee card this is a lot of support. He's tanky enough to hold a melee slot, and he provides a competitive amount of support from that slot. :card[734] is stronger where he fits, but his 25% crit rate is leader-locked, and :card[2477] has a better skill but can't match his stacking in long runs. He's still one of the best support melees in the game, and for newer players he's about as good as it gets: strong support on one copy, a body that can tank, and he only costs event points. **Get at least one, 3 if Phantasma is your main team** — one of the best support melees out there and a great pickup for new players, and this is the cheapest he'll ever be.

## TL;DR

Event Cards:
- :card[739] — **Aim for 2 copies, a great reserve for new players** — same skill as :card[1720], plus 25% crit DMG to your top two every wave from 2 copies, for any attribute. Behind :card[1720] in short fights, ahead from wave 3 on, so she shines in multi-round battles. Her leader ability is level 90, so she's a support and reserve, not a lead
- :card[738] — **Get at least one, 3 for Phantasma mains** — one of the best support melees out there and a great pickup for new players. F2P Phantasma staple, crit DMG stacking every wave on one copy, final wave team DMG at 3. Best in multi-wave content, where his random targeting has time to find the right cards.

3rd Anniversary Gacha (full write-ups in the [Anniversary Special Auction](/blog/anniversary-auction-september-2026); these picks are for the mochi exchange):
- :card[734] — **Worth a buy for most players who don't have him** — one copy for 200 mochi is cheap for the option to flex 25% Phantasma crit rate, since his leader ability is live at base level
- :card[733] — **Better value for mochi if you need a Divina leader** — his 50% crit damage leader ability comes online at one copy, so 200 mochi gets you a lot of up-front value
- :card[742] — **Good buy for mochi** — EXP stacks across assists, so shes a good option. She can only go on a team once, so you only need one unlocked copy, 3 copies for 50 mochi each she is probably also fine to 4* orb if you want to save mochi
- :card[740] — **Ok buy if you missed :card[1651]** — solid Phantasma multi-wave carry for conquest and endless, but the per-wave damage is 4 limit breaks away, so she's probably not worth orbing now unless you're very fresh
- :card[741] — **Probably not a buy for mochi** — she takes too many copies for full power, the exchange is limit 1, and she's probably not worth orbing for most players, especially if you have any cards from 4th anniversary to orb instead
- :card[732] — **Skip** — :card[228] and :card[2253] lead better; only worth it if you want her for the event point helper boost, and 200 mochi is expensive for that
