Hope people are enjoying a bit of a slower event with the 3rd anniversary re-run, it was kind of nice not to worry about rankings.  This month is Alice in Ayakashi Land, :card[1413] from exchange, and I think we will see The Definitive Eating Competition for Spirits!

## Notable Picks

:card[1578] — is a staple for Divina world boss teams, and a premium Divina support option.  Her skill is half of a :card[1720] or :card[739], which makes her a low priority for your lineup if you are running either of those, however her 45% damage to the top 2 Divina ATK is excellent, and the 10% time extend is good to boot, especially in world boss where top teams are starting to cap damage, so time extension is a small multiplier but one that ignores damage caps.  She's a buy for Divina players, and only Divina players because of the attribute restriction on her first ability, and an option to buy and seal if you want to get some of your jewels back, as she is most likely to want to go into a reserve slot anyway.

**Buy for Divina** — 45% DMG to your top two Divina plus 10% battle time that ignores damage caps. A world boss staple that lives happily in a reserve slot.

:card[1638] — When I first covered this card I think I didn't see the value here, and I still think the card is a little underbudget, but there aren't a lot of options for damage resistance assists, and it's the strongest passive damage amp assist by a fair margin.  The underbudget part is when compared to lineup cards like :card[1631], 25% damage amp and 10% DR here are less than what you get for an in lineup card slot.  It isn't necessarily fair to compare one for one assists vs cards because they clearly don't budget the same, but if we looked at :card[1129], 35% DMG is pretty similar to what you might get from a card like :card[1051], :card[1992], :card[1528].  So just getting 10% less damage amp means you are losing overall budget that could be going toward something else.  Passive damage amp is a decent multiplier, but if you run a carry that applies damage amp like :card[1401] then it's likely that those are capping damage amp and during the active debuff you don't get anything.  With that in mind in those setups you probably have it helping half or less of your damage, so a 25% damage amp might really be adding 12% or so overall as a multiplier.  When you don't it's good, :card[1526] teams for example lack that and so it's a pretty solid choice for a 25% damage multiplier.  Ok so that's a lot to say there aren't a lot of options if you want a damage amp assist, and the DR is a nice add on top of it.  This one is a buy if you don't run active damage amps, and could be nice for utility, but not really required otherwise.

**Buy if your team has no active damage amp** — the best passive amp assist with 10% DR on top, but a :card[1401] style carry already caps amp and leaves her doing much less.

## Alice in Ayakashi Land

### Event Cards (permanent)

:card[1576] — another 3 target skill damage with 15% skill damage per wave, this time with a small self DR, which is awkward on ranged cards.  It makes her tankier, but :card[1402] is still the best defensive choice in this niche, and :card[2248] is the strongest offensive option, but for new players you can buy the cheapest option between :card[1576], :card[1402], or :card[1435] as a placeholder if you want.

**Placeholder only** — buy the cheapest of :card[1576], :card[1402] and :card[1435] if you need one.

:card[1577] — another (wild) melee option.  Maintaining the condition can be a pain so :card[1539] is probably the better passive option, but again new players can go with something cheap like :card[1577] to act as a tank until you get something better.

**Placeholder only** — a cheap tank for new players, :card[1539] is the better passive option.

:card[1579] — not a super strong ranking option, but she can be nice for some players.  Her skill is good if you need something to cap crit rate in shorter fights or don't have a progressive crit rate booster.  Her passives are ok, attack speed is useful, and with options in Divina it can be possible to stack a lot of attack speed which is helpful for auto attack damage.  Her progressive crit damage is nice in longer fights, but is expensive at 5 copies.  This might be one to watch the price on for newer players who are building up Divina, especially if you need a way to ensure you cap your skills by critting.

**Watch the price if you're building Divina** — a crit rate skill and team attack speed, but the crit damage needs 5 copies.

:card[1581] — is the half-ranking reward and a bit underpowered for a passive healer.  The damage buff on the first ability is nice, but that could just be part of the skill.  Cleanse is a bit niche, it can be useful for some world bosses, but it comes with weaker support abilities.  30% crit damage is also a bit underpowered for 5 copies.  This might be an ok passive healer for newer players that are looking to improve the support from their healer, but isn't a good option for stronger players.

**Skip unless you're newer** — cleanse is niche and 30% crit DMG is light for 5 copies.

## Exchange Card

:card[1413] — :card[722] is getting lower in stock but is the better option for 10% crit rate for Anima from the melee slot.  She does offer some support from the Anima melee slot so that may be ok as a placeholder if the stock/cost is better than :card[722].  :card[2250] is probably the premium comparison, but he requires the leader slot, and he won't return for some time anyway.

**Placeholder** — same 10% Anima crit rate as :card[722], who does it better. Buy her only if she's cheaper or :card[722] runs out.

## Rerun: The Definitive Eating Competition for Spirits!

:card[709] — is an ok placeholder carry, when she came out she was an attempt at a Phantasma answer to :card[630], that may have fallen a bit short.  She trades attack speed for progressive self crit damage, that can be nice in the modern game with progressive crit rate.  Probably not a top choice but if the price is right she could be an ok filler until you are able to get :card[1651] for multi wave or :card[1401] for any battles.

**Filler if cheap** — a stopgap Phantasma carry until :card[1651] or :card[1401].

:card[710] — 10 levels and 20% skill damage to Divina, this is an ok but awkward set of bonuses.  The levels kind of aim toward wanting this on a damage dealer, but the skill damage wants to go on a support.  10 levels is a fairly low multiplier so this is more for the skill damage, which is also pretty low here.  It's not bad but there are better options than the 5 copies needed here.

**Skip** — awkward, low value bonuses for 5 copies.
