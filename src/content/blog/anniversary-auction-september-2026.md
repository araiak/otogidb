We have the anniversary auction, but this time around with most of the cards they have been powercrept except for :card[550], maybe even in this event, but depending on stock and where you are at in your team building there are some solid picks, especially if you don't have a lot of invokers on hand. ## Notable Picks

:card[550] is a staple assist and probably the best pickup, although pricing may be high as stock wanes over time.  She provides 50% speed and 10% time extend.  She is the only time extending assist so that in its own is interesting, time extends while not all that strong are a great way to just add 10% more clock time, which is more orbs, and more time to do damage.  She is a solid pickup, but 4 copies may be a stretch depending on pricing.  **Top pick of the auction — buy, but know what you're paying for.** The 50% ATK speed is live on a single copy, the 10% time extend requires LB4. One copy is worth it either way; only chase the full set if the price stays low, and remember you can do an assist flip if you can unlock and seal to sell back.

:card[553] is a solid support carry.  He has a damage amp skill and 25% crit damage and 25% damage to the top two.  This isn't by itself amazing, when divina also hosts a number of cards that have high damage to the top two.  A lack of damage amp skills in divina make him usable, but probably not in the best teams, but if the price is right hes a solid builder if you don't have better supports or don't have a lot of damage skills on your team.  The biggest problem here divina is pretty crowded if you are running :card[1721]/:card[1720] pairing with a :card[1721] helper you don't have a lot of room left in your team.  He is a good pick in multi round format where :card[1721] abilities turn on too late as a carry, but :card[1311] from the auction is not much worse, so you have to decide if he offers enough for your team to justify the price.  **Buy if the price is right, skip otherwise** — a fine budget Divina support if you're short on damage amp, but both of his buffs are limit break gated and the attribute is crowded at the top.

:card[740] is being replaced by :card[1651] in pretty much everyway, but you might not have 4 orbs or a stack of invokers to drop on her.  :card[740] is close to my heart as my first real carry and is solid in conquest due to damage amp and damage per round, plus a nice small skill damage boost on top.  He is also a staple in endless phantasma setups.  So yeah, if you don't plan to go all in on :card[1651] and you are looking for a phantasma multi rounder to tide you over, look no further.  **Buy if you're not going all in on :card[1651]** — a good stopgap phantasma multirounder for conquest and endless, but it's a stopgap, and the per-wave damage needs a full 4 limit breaks.

:card[734] is also a staple for phantasma conquest, and a good all around phantasma leader.  I mentioned in my analysis of :card[2250] the leader slot is going to be highly contested by :card[1650] even off type, but :card[734] is not far behind in overall power, and can enable some conquest strategies.  The nice part is his leader ability is unlocked at base level, so a single copy is all you need for him to be useful.  **Strong buy for phantasma, and one copy is all you need** — the cheapest real leader in this auction, and the best value pickup here after :card[550].

:card[742] is a 4* but is a 10% XP boost assist, if you don't have her and you didn't stock up on a bunch of :card[614] to kit out your XP farming team, if you have one, she is a solid pickup, though note her ability doesn't unlock until level 70 and she caps at 60, so you need two limit breaks before she does anything.  You can 4* orb her to get there if you don't want to spend much.  **Buy one if you run an XP team, skip if you don't** — the EXP boost stacks across assists so she stacks with other XP assists.

## 2nd Anniversary

:card[536] / :card[537] / :card[538] are all attribute leads that have been thouroughly powercrept, the 3rd anni versions are generally better, and all of them should be cheaper.  Part of why is unlock cost: the 3rd anni leader abilities all come online at base level, while these three don't unlock until LB2, with 2000 minimum thats 6000, where you can probably get any of the 3rd anniversary cards for less.  Plus getting that many copies may be hard and they aren't worth orbing.  Grab if you want for the collection, but not for your leader.  **Collection only** — the 3rd anniversary versions are better and cheaper to switch on.

:card[551] is 12% crit which is easy enough to ignore for :card[2238] if thats something you are looking for.  :card[2238] will be avaliable for mochi near the end of the event.  **Skip** — :card[2238] is nearly double the crit rate for the same 2 limit breaks, and costs mochi instead of cash.

:card[552] is 30% crit damage and is weaker than either :card[723] or :card[643] from auction so don't waste your money unless you want it for your collection.  **Skip** — :card[723] and :card[643] both beat it and hit your top two instead of just themselves.

:card[554] is 3*, enough said.  **Skip.**

## 3rd Anniversary

:card[732] is 25% DMG to anima and 15% progressive skill damage, as leader for new players :card[228] or :card[2253] are probably better value and only cost mochi.  Also with :card[2250] or :card[1650] in the current event, those are better targets for anima players.  **Skip** — :card[228] and :card[2253] cost mochi instead of cash and do the leader job better, and the current event has stronger anima targets anyway.

:card[733] is one of the better Divina melee options, he brings a damage skill which there aren't a lot of in divina but no damage amp which is hobbled by his low crit rate.  His 50% crit damage to Divina as leader and 15% progressive skill damage to Divina are pretty good, but he may struggle to tank being very ATK heavy with low HP.  He and :card[724] are good divina melee, and only :card[733] is avaliable.  So hes an ok melee and leader, but he also suffers from :card[1650] being better and working across attributes.  **Only worth it if he's actually leading your Divina team** — the 50% crit damage is the card, and it's a leader ability, so off-leader you're left with a body and a single target skill on 7.5% base crit.  If :card[1650] is taking your divina leader slot, there's nothing here.  But if you are running a full divina team, hes a good option.

:card[741] is the best single target buffer in the game, but this is niche which has mostly been left behind for 2 carry teams.  She buffs three stats where :card[1735] only buffs two, which puts her ahead overall, though :card[1735] is still the strongest single target buffer for phantasma specifically.  Neither of her abilities is attribute locked, so she slots into any team regardless of what you're running, which makes her a solid midgame healer for pretty much anyone.  She is still a solid upgrade for a lot of players and will probably go for close to minimum, so cheap next to pulling for something.  She requires 5 copies for full power, which is probably a stretch for a cash card, but if you need an extra healer to improve your teams shes a pretty solid choice until you have a solid 2 carry setup.  **Best value pickup for midgame players — buy one** — no attribute lock means she improves any team you're running, and she should go for close to minimum. Don't chase all 5 copies unless you're committed.

## TL;DR

Notable Picks:
- :card[550] — **Top pick of the auction** — 50% ATK speed on a single copy plus the only time extend on an assist. The extend is level 85 against a level 70 cap so it needs 4 copies; one copy is still worth it for the speed alone
- :card[734] — **Strong buy for phantasma, one copy is enough** — leader ability is live at base level, making him the cheapest real leader here. Behind :card[1650] for raw power but enables conquest strategies
- :card[741] — **Best value for midgame players, buy one** — no attribute lock, so she slots into any team, and she should go for close to minimum. Three stat buffs beats :card[1735]'s two, though :card[1735] stays the strongest phantasma-specific option. Full power is 5 copies, don't chase it
- :card[740] — **Buy if you're not going all in on :card[1651]** — solid phantasma multirounder for conquest and endless, but a stopgap, and the per-wave damage is 4 limit breaks away
- :card[742] — **Buy 3 if you run an XP team** — EXP stacks across assists so she works alongside :card[614], but her ability is level 70 against a level 60 cap, so budget three copies or the orbs
- :card[553] — **Buy if the price is right** — budget Divina support if you're short on damage amp, but both buffs are limit break gated and Divina is crowded at the top

2nd Anniversary:
- :card[536] / :card[537] / :card[538] — **Collection only** — attribute leaders that need 2 limit breaks to do anything at all, where the 3rd anniversary versions work at base level for less
- :card[551] — **Skip** — :card[2238] is nearly double the crit rate for the same 2 limit breaks and costs mochi
- :card[552] — **Skip** — :card[723] and :card[643] beat it and buff your top two instead of themselves
- :card[554] — **Skip** — 3 star, collection only

3rd Anniversary:
- :card[732] — **Skip** — :card[228] and :card[2253] cost mochi instead of cash and lead better; the current event has stronger anima targets
- :card[733] — **Only buy if he's leading your Divina team** — the 50% crit damage is a leader ability and it's most of the card, live on one copy. Off-leader he's just a body, and :card[1650] can take that slot across attributes
