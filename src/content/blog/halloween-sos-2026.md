## Notable Picks

:card[1918] is a great support or reserve, it combines the best parts of :card[672] with the targeting of :card[739] and has basically no attribute locking.  She's especially strong as a reserve in multi round content: either ability would be playable on its own, 20% crit DMG stacking every wave or a flat 45% DMG to the top 2, and having both together is insane value for any 1-2 carry team, whatever the attribute.  Her skill is a smaller version of :card[1720]'s DMG buff (the heal isn't really the draw), which is a nice filler if you don't have :card[1720] or :card[739], and in the latter case her skill plus the 45% from her ability nearly breaks even with :card[1720]'s skill over its 8s duration (roughly 80% + 45% vs 125%), while that 45% keeps working outside the skill window as well.  Likely an LJS or an overall ranking reward, and probably worth pushing for most players.  **Great pickup for most players, a standout multi-round reserve** — 20% crit DMG stacking each wave and 45% DMG to the 2 highest-ATK allies with no attribute lock; either alone is playable, together they're insane value for any 1-2 carry team, and her skill plus ability nearly matches :card[1720]'s skill window.

## Limited Jewel Summons

:card[2477] is a solid Phantasma melee support: 40% crit DMG to the 2 highest-ATK allies on entry, 45% skill DMG and DMG dealt to herself on the final wave, and a single-target skill that can NUMB for 8 seconds. She is a direct upgrade to :card[383] on every axis — more ATK, more HP, a bigger skill, 40% crit DMG instead of 30%, 45% instead of 40% on the final wave — and cheap to switch on, since level 75 is one limit break and the team-facing ability is live at 2 copies, and that is the ability you most want here. MLB only gates the self buff, which is nice, but was never going to carry a 7.5% base crit melee anyway.

The ceiling is real though: 40% to two allies doesn't reach :card[1720]'s 50%, and 4★ options like :card[600] or :card[1527] do the same 40% to 44% from reserve for free. What she has going for her is if you were going to slot a melee to help tank, she provides all that crit damage from your melee card slot. Phantasma melee support usually means :card[734] or :card[738]. :card[734] is the strongest of the bunch, but her 25% crit rate is leader-locked, so she can't slot alongside :card[1650]. :card[738] is the closer comparison — he stacks 25% crit DMG to 2 random Phantasma allies every wave, adds 20% Phantasma team DMG on the final wave, and is tankier, so he wins the long runs. :card[2477] trades that team damage for a better skill, more offense, and a buff that locks onto the allies you built around at entry instead of hoping it hits the right card each wave. I don't think that's enough to pull a standard gacha melee, but if you end up with copies she should find use situationally. **Strong Phantasma melee, not worth chasing** — a straight upgrade to :card[383] that's useful from 2 copies, but under :card[1720] and matched by 4★ :card[600]; :card[734] is better where she fits and :card[738] usually wins multi-wave battles, leaving her a situational pick if copies fall in your lap.

## Event Cards

:card[1915] is a bit of an underpowered leader card.  He loses to :card[724] for team support, which is 30% damage and 30% attack speed, his attack speed is personal and the team damage is only 25%.  The best leaders are still :card[1650], and :card[724], or even :card[733] or :card[739] can be used if you don't have them, :card[1915] just doesn't offer enough to justify the leader slot if you have any other option.  **Skip** — underpowered leader; 25% team DMG loses to :card[724]'s 30% DMG plus 30% team ATK speed, and :card[1650], :card[724], :card[733] or :card[739] all justify the slot better.

:card[1916] is an interesting one, her skill hits the 2 enemies with the highest ATK, which is an awkward spot between wave clear and a big single target skill.  The damage-taken debuff is nice, but she's a little awkward in situations where you want one or the other; in story or tower this is probably fine, but it potentially keeps her more of a reserve option unless you need the debuff.  Her 15% damage every wave goes to 2 random Phantasma allies, not the top 2 ATK, so it isn't quite :card[1651] or :card[1731] where they hit the whole team, and it isn't guaranteed to land on your carries either.  It's still helpful, but the randomness cuts into her value as a progressive pick, since in multi round fights it may not go where you want it.  Her 20% crit rate is also fixed regardless of fight length, so in multiwave :card[1651], :card[1672], or even :card[1307] match her on the second round and pass her on the third, and :card[1651] is clearly the better option from there.  In shorter battles like world boss she's competitive with :card[1669] or :card[734], but between the random targeting and slightly less crit rate she isn't really better than :card[1669].  She's also an option to cap crit for :card[1401] teams instead of :card[734] or an assist like :card[697] — :card[1401]'s 52.5% plus her 20% and :card[1669]'s 25% final wave lands at 97.5% — but running both Dragon Princesses for their second abilities may give up too much against stronger support options like :card[1051].  She does make it a little easier to run :card[1650] as leader instead of :card[734] for crit rate, but if you have :card[1669] and :card[1651] or :card[1672] you probably don't need her.  She's fine, and she could make a multi round lineup or a world boss lineup, but she probably isn't the top pick in either niche.  **Fine, but not the top pick in her niche — notable if you don't have :card[1669] and the crit gets fixed** — 15% DMG to 2 random Phantasma allies each wave plus a flat 20% crit rate; :card[1651] wins multi-wave from round 3 and :card[1669] matches her in world boss; a crit-cap option for :card[1401] teams, and her awkward 2-target skill keeps her more of a reserve.

:card[1917] is your standard [Wild] card, this time we get 40% damage reduction and 60% damage under 50% health.  The HP threshold is too hard to maintain, and new players would be better off with :card[738] especially with current prices, sell to buy :card[738].  This should be the tower drop.  **Skip** — 40% DR and 60% DMG under half HP is too hard to maintain; sell to buy :card[738].

:card[1921] is somewhat similar to :card[1317] from last year, she trades :card[1317]'s 10% damage reduction for 5% extra skill damage per round and a round start heal.  These are both fine for new players, but nothing special, and she might be the first-half ranking reward the same as last year.  **Fine for new players, nothing special** — trades :card[1317]'s 10% DR for 5% more skill DMG per round and a round-start heal.

:card[1922] is a clone of :card[1310] but for Anima, this should be a similar value to :card[1310] or :card[1129] but the latter has no type restrictions and damage reduction scales a bit better than HP, generally.  My guess is LJS for this one, and it's probably worth at least the mochi copy, but if you have :card[1129] unlocked, then its probably the better option of the two, however Anima also has less support options so this could see some space in lineups, although between :card[1129], :card[656] for mochi, there are a lot of % damage options so you may not need to go so deep as to use this card for assists, unless you are lacking assists, or those other options.  **Worth the mochi copy if you're short on Anima assists** — an Anima :card[1310] with 35% final-wave DMG; :card[1129] is the better option if unlocked, and :card[656] covers the slot too.

Likely to be fixed.  The HP probably is the weaker part, if you are investing it's for the 35% damage anyway."]

:card[1928] is comparable to :card[1584] or :card[1773] — all three give 15% skill damage every wave, but :card[1928]'s goes to the whole team with no attribute lock, where the other two are Divina only.  On top of that he adds 10 levels to all allies, which is a better rider than :card[1773]'s 4% HP per wave, though :card[1584] is a hard comparison since she's generally only run for EXP/drop rate.  Against the self-buffing progressive skill cards we already have — :card[1435], :card[2248], :card[1402], :card[1576] this month, :card[1609] next month — he offers a bit more, since the skill damage fans out to the team instead of only feeding himself.  Not a super strong card, but being a little less greedy could be nice on a wave clearer.  Probably not worth heavy investment, especially if it turns out to be an LJS.  **Not worth heavy investment** — 15% skill DMG each wave to the whole team with no attribute lock plus +10 levels to all allies; a little less greedy than :card[1584], :card[1773] or the self-buffers, nice on a wave clearer.

The 4★ and 3★ cards aren't really worth mentioning this time around.
