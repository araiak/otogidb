### Limited Jewel Summons

:card[2477] is a solid Phantasma melee support: 40% crit DMG to the 2 highest-ATK allies on entry, 45% skill DMG and DMG dealt to herself on the final wave, and a single-target skill that can NUMB for 8 seconds. She is a direct upgrade to :card[383] on every axis — more ATK, more HP, a bigger skill, 40% crit DMG instead of 30%, 45% instead of 40% on the final wave — and cheap to switch on, since level 75 is one limit break and the team-facing ability is live at 2 copies, and that is the ability you most want here. MLB only gates the self buff, which is nice, but was never going to carry a 7.5% base crit melee anyway.

The ceiling is real though: 40% to two allies doesn't reach :card[1720]'s 50%, and 4★ options like :card[600] or :card[1527] do the same 40% to 44% from reserve for free. What she has going for her is if you were going to slot a melee to help tank, she provides all that crit damage from your melee card slot. Phantasma melee support usually means :card[734] or :card[738]. :card[734] is the strongest of the bunch, but her 25% crit rate is leader-locked, so she can't slot alongside :card[1650]. :card[738] is the closer comparison — he stacks 25% crit DMG to 2 random Phantasma allies every wave, adds 20% Phantasma team DMG on the final wave, and is tankier, so he wins the long runs. :card[2477] trades that team damage for a better skill, more offense, and a buff that locks onto the allies you built around at entry instead of hoping it hits the right card each wave. I don't think that's enough to pull a standard gacha melee, but if you end up with copies she should find use situationally. **Strong Phantasma melee, not worth chasing** — a straight upgrade to :card[383] that's useful from 2 copies, but under :card[1720] and matched by 4★ :card[600]; :card[734] is better where she fits and :card[738] usually wins multi-wave battles, leaving her a situational pick if copies fall in your lap.
