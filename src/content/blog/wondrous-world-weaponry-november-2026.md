## Notable Picks


:card[1680] might be the star of the event.  A progressive self damage wave clearer with XP boost, yes please.  The only downside here is XP teams usually run Divina to use :card[1494]'s progressive crit rate, and she's Phantasma, but at least she brings her own with 25% always on crit rate, and 20% XP to boot.  There are a lot of healers with XP boost, but only a few ranged attackers, and she is arguably one of the best options.  The next best option is :card[644] who can get her crit from :card[1494] and brings 15% ATK speed to the whole team, but :card[1680] has higher ATK, hits more targets, and increases her own damage to boot, so on damage it's not close.  She's one of the better picks of the event and probably one of the easier XP picks.  A lot of players will probably buy her, but you may not see a lot of her as a helper, because :card[1488] offers 25% XP to her 20%, and a lot of players will probably keep :card[1488] as their helper if they have her.  XP is a niche, but if you want a ranged XP booster you probably need to invest in your own copy rather than borrow one.  **Great XP pick, worth grabbing** — a progressive self damage wave clearer with 25% crit rate and 20% XP that out-damages :card[644], but expect to own your copy since most helpers will run :card[1488]'s 25% XP.

:card[1827] is one of the standouts here.  Anima is a bit lacking in good Anima specific support assists.  :card[1922] was an underwhelming add to address that, but :card[1827] closes the gap and is a top tier Anima support assist.  This card is just better than :card[656] which has been a staple for Anima teams for some time.  It goes from 25% DMG in the final round to 30%, which is competitive with :card[1531] in world boss or close to :card[1129].  Its skill damage goes from 25% to 40%, and since they are different abilities you can run both and stack them for 65% skill damage.  Anima doesn't really have any standouts for skill damage beyond :card[656].  :card[1526] teams want a lot of skill damage because his base skill damage is very low relative to his ATK value.  This card probably will find its way into most Anima world boss lineups either in addition to or replacing :card[656].  This one is probably a buy for players who want to focus on Anima, and worth at least the mochi copy for everyone who can afford it assuming it is from the LJS.  **Buy for Anima, mochi copy for everyone** — a straight upgrade on :card[656] with 30% final round DMG and 40% skill DMG, stacks with it for 65% skill DMG, and should land in most Anima world boss lineups.

## Limited Jewel Summons

:card[1789] brings a flat 25% damage to Divina, a skill that cleanses 3 allies, and 5% ATK speed per round, which reminds me a small bit of :card[1611].  That ATK speed stacks each round, so over 5 rounds it's ok and over 10 rounds it's pretty decent, which makes it worthwhile in endless but too weak to be very useful in most other formats.  For comparison, :card[1321] gives a flat 25% ATK speed to Divina from the first round, so :card[1789] only catches up at round 5 and doubles it by round 10, and :card[1321] adds 40% damage to the 2 Divina allies with the highest ATK on top.  MLB is a big investment for a healer that is just ok in longer fights or endless.  Not one to invest a lot in, but collect her over time if you get copies and she could see some use.  **Collect over time, don't invest** — flat 25% Divina DMG, a 3 ally cleanse and 5% ATK speed per round that only catches :card[1321] at round 5; good in endless, but MLB is a lot for a healer.

## 5★ Cards


:card[1816] is an ok Anima attacker.  She is similar to :card[674] in that her single target damage down skill isn't quite what you want, but she packs a lot of self buffs, none of which are self crit rate.  That cuts her top end performance significantly, and makes her just ok.  :card[1309] is the other close comparison, another self buffing Anima ranged with no crit rate of her own, but her 90% crit damage depends on having :card[1308] on the team, where :card[1816]'s 50% crit damage and 40% damage need nothing.  It kind of depends where the card comes from but it might be an ok Anima filler for newer players, if it's easy enough to get.  **Anima filler for newer players if easy to get** — big self buffs with no dependency like :card[1309], but no crit rate and an awkward skill cap her top end.

:card[1819] isn't quite the rumored carry, but she is a pretty solid card for multi round battles.  She brings high attack, a damage amp skill, 28% less damage from all foes each wave, and 15% crit rate per round, which means in round 1 the 15% makes up for her lower melee crit rate, and by the end of 5+ rounds she should be rocking a fairly good crit rate.  She might be able to find a place as a defensive support for :card[1526]/:card[1529] teams, since those teams run limited damage skills and lack damage amp in general, and her skill brings exactly that.  The 15% crit rate in a single round probably makes her too weak for world boss, but she could be a competitive tank in story or conquest.

Anima melee is pretty limited to :card[2250] who competes with :card[1650] for the leader slot, :card[690] for defensive single round value, :card[1498] who has more crit rate but less utility, and :card[1438] who is the closest match.  :card[1438] gets progressive damage, 20% per wave, where :card[1819] gets progressive crit rate, 15% per wave, and :card[1438]'s 25% damage down only hits the enemy with the highest ATK, while :card[1819]'s 28% hits all foes.  If you run :card[1784] or :card[1672] for crit rate then :card[1438] is probably the better utility/tank.  Otherwise :card[1819] is the better pick, especially on :card[1526]/:card[1529] teams, where :card[1784] or :card[1672] are a bit overkill anyway.  **Solid multi-round tank, not a carry** — damage amp skill, 28% less damage from all foes each wave and 15% crit rate per round; a good fit for :card[1526]/:card[1529] teams, :card[1438] wins if you run :card[1784] or :card[1672], and too weak for world boss.

:card[1826] looks like a potential story card, your standard melee card that is mostly underwhelming.  She is skill damage focused instead of defensive, which could be ok for newer players if your team is strong enough to not need DR on your frontline.  The closest comparison is :card[1465] with 40% self skill damage, and :card[1826]'s 60% is just a better option.  Assuming she's an event card, :card[1465] is a cheap mochi card, so she'll likely end up a slightly better cheap mochi card when she hits auction, fine as a Divina melee filler.  Of the two Divina melee in this event, :card[1830] is the better option, although he may be LJS or ranking.  **Divina melee filler** — 60% self skill DMG beats :card[1465], so she's a slightly better cheap mochi card; :card[1830] is the better Divina melee in the event.

:card[1828] is the other candidate for the rumored Anima carry, but her kit is kind of all over the place, and she ends up more of a decent support and wave clearer than a carry.  In a world boss lineup she'd be there for her buffs, not her skill: high ATK, 35% crit damage to Anima team members, and 50% self damage that gives her auto attacks a small boost to boot.  She could also be used as a reserve in teams where transitioning from 2 target buffs to team buffs provides a lot of value, and her first ability, the 35% crit damage to Anima that matters in reserve, unlocks at 2 copies, so that may not be all that expensive depending on the source.

The closest reserve comparison is :card[228], whose 30% crit damage to Anima is the same kind of buff, but her leader skill is usually displaced by :card[1650], so :card[1828]'s 35% is a straight improvement there.  :card[600] gives a larger 40% crit damage, but only to the 2 allies with the highest ATK, and trading that for 35% to every Anima ally is probably a good trade.  It's the same idea as :card[643] vs :card[564] for assists: your top 2 are usually buffed very heavily already, so another 2 target buff adds very little to them, while your 3rd, 4th and 5th attackers often start from a very low base, so a team buff gives them a much larger increase.  She probably isn't strong enough to matter much though.  She's a small gain over cards that are ok, but not great, so she's playable in a few different situations without being the best card for any of them.  Worth a look if she's an event reward, but not one to chase.  **Ok, best as an Anima reserve** — 35% crit DMG to all Anima at 2 copies is a small gain over :card[228] and :card[600], but those are ok cards, not great ones, and she isn't the rumored carry.

:card[1830] is interesting.  He's a bit of a poor man's :card[725]: the same defensive slow, but greedy self buffs instead of :card[725]'s big skill damage support, on a low ATK base.  His 45% crit rate and 45% crit damage mostly make up for his melee base crit, but not his low ATK.  Slows are extremely strong in world boss: a slow from a lineup card makes the boss skip every other cast of its big single target skills, and that means less CC/stuns and incoming damage.  It's a better defensive tool than plain damage reduction, which is why :card[725] often beats something like :card[1631] defensively.  It tops out at half of those casts, so he adds nothing if you already run :card[725] or :card[690], but for Divina teams without one he's a defensive lineup option, although the cost is very high.

The problem is the top teams are mostly 2 carry teams, and :card[725]'s 60% skill damage to the top 2 is worth more than :card[1830]'s individual contribution.  He may struggle to do enough damage to become a third carry, even if 3 carry teams become a thing.  Divina also has very strong 2 target buffs like :card[1720] or :card[723], which makes it harder to add a 3rd damage dealer without losing power, but that is probably a direction teams need to push due to damage caps.  If :card[1615]'s 25% crit rate went to the whole Divina team like :card[1669]'s does for Phantasma, instead of just the top 2, or if he had a higher ATK value, he might be a more competitive option.  He has the right look, and maybe players find a way to make it work, so I'd grab the mochi copy.  He might pay off if Divina's buffs shift from 2 target to the whole team faster than his 45%/45% gets replaced by a better 3rd option.  MLB is a big damage buff on a low damage card, but he fills the defensive role at 2 copies, and he's probably not worth chasing if he's LJS.  **Grab the mochi copy** — the right look as a Divina slow and possible 3rd carry, but low ATK; he pays off if Divina buffs move from 2 target to team wide before a better 3rd option replaces his 45%/45%.

:card[2016] is an interesting one, it's a worse :card[680] in the final round, but decent otherwise.  In multi round formats it's tempting to take her over :card[680] since her 25% works every round, but the last round is typically a boss with the most HP, where :card[680]'s 40% matters most, so on actual clear speed it's kind of a toss up.  You can run both, but two assist slots is a lot for 65% ATK speed.  Keep an eye on the cost for this one, if it's exchange or easy to get, it's probably worth grabbing, and it's only 2 copies for the unlock.  **Watch the cost, grab if cheap** — 25% team ATK speed every round against :card[680]'s 40% in the final round is a toss up on clear speed, and only 2 copies to unlock.

## 4★ and 3★ Cards

:card[1808], :card[1818] and :card[1823] are probably helpers, so get them for the helper ability if you want that, not for the card.

:card[1808] gives 5% damage per wave.  It is usable in endless or multi-round if you don't have a full set of assists, but it is well below :card[728], and there are lots of cards of equal value out there.  Even :card[728] isn't really used outside of endless.

:card[1818] gives 13% lifesteal.  Keep it around if you don't have a lifesteal assist.  It falls short of :card[1430] at 15%, but the difference is small and lifesteal can be useful on a tank to self-sustain in low SE cost teams.

:card[1823] gives 11% max HP and 12% DR.  Small DR and a small HP boost, this is ok for new players if you need to increase a tank's health pool, but probably not worth keeping for most players after the event.

:card[1824] heals 3% HP every 5 seconds to the 2 allies with the highest ATK.  That is usually your backline, not your tanks, so the heal doesn't do much, and it's not an assist so it's taking up a card slot too.  Easy sell after the event.

:card[1825] gives 12% drop rate, so this one might be worth keeping, especially if it's a cheap event card.  Most places you would use drop rate, you can also benefit from XP, so I'd probably use XP first and this only on stages you've already cleared and don't need XP from.  But there aren't a lot of drop rate assists, so hang onto a copy.

:card[1811] gives 7% damage.  Easy sell after the event, probably not worth putting too many resources into leveling for too little gain.
