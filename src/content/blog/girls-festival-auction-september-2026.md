## Notable Picks

:card[1526] is a relatively strong crit rate buffer who has a hard requirement of pairing with :card[1529] who we will discuss here as well.  While I think he is the strongest carry in Anima, but despite that his skill makes him hard to build around and makes the overall power of the pairing lower than :card[1401] or the :card[1721] / :card[1720] pairing.  The biggest strengths are his total buff when paired with :card[1529] 40% Crit Rate, 50% Crit Damage, and 60% Damage in the final round.  This is the strongest of the three as a total package.  :card[1529] also brings 15% all-ally crit DMG per wave and another 20% crit rate bringing :card[1526] to a grand total of 80%.  She is great in multiround content and  fine in one round like World Boss where she is similar power to :card[1669] or :card[1615] but spreading her slightly smaller buffs more widely.  The big downside is :card[1526]'s skill and a lack of Anima specific supports.  :card[1526]'s skill decreases SPD instead of giving a damage amp like :card[1401] and :card[1721] this is a fairly large downside, :card[1526] also not having a damage amp means 2 slots are already getting that additional damage increase.  In capped content 8 seconds of 75% damage increase per 20 seconds is 24% damage increase and often during your burst, this is a big loss.  That also forces your hand on team building, but the cards that might fill that gap aren't as strong as Divina or Phantasma supports, and many of the best supports for Anima are not attribute locked, so they also increase the power of :card[1401] or :card[1721] like :card[1650] or :card[514].  If you search for abilities tagged multi from Anima you will find 3 5* cards, Anima tends to have wide and small buffs for Anima, not 2 target buffs that drive the meta.  Despite that both cards are a fairly good buy, a lack of strong supports is something that could change and make these cards worthwhile.  The pairing is also strong enough for any content despite not being the strongest team available.  This pair is a buy for players who want to play Anima or want to be ready if the meta shifts toward Anima.

### Limited Jewel Summons

:card[1526] is the other Limited Jewel Summon this month — see Notable Picks above.

:card[1540] is a progressive crit rate card that is comparable to :card[1494].  The comparison is a bit difficult.  :card[1540] gives 10% crit rate per round, and some free HP on top of it, where :card[1494] gives 7% crit rate per round that gets 20% XP and 30% soulstones.  Both are limited to multi wave fights.  :card[1540] is the stronger card, but multi wave fights would cover Conquest and Special Story.  In Conquest :card[1540] is the winner, there's no XP, but in Special Story to most players XP is more important than the extra crit for sustain.  :card[1540]'s skill is also arguably better for new players despite its own anti-synergy with herself.  In any case if you care about the XP in Special Story, :card[1540] is very expensive for a very modest gain that you won't use most of the time anyway.  If you care about min maxing or don't have :card[1494] or :card[1672] you can buy :card[1540], but if you already have :card[1494] or :card[1672] you are probably fine skipping this one.

## Event Rewards

:card[1529] is the overall ranking reward and is covered with :card[1526] in Notable Picks above.

:card[1528] is a surprisingly usable half ranking reward.  Her skill is not great, but her abilities are competitive for Divina reserve especially in short battles.  She probably falls behind the top tier supports like :card[1578] or :card[1321], but 35% damage in the final round is competitive in short fights, and 25% attack speed is a nice bonus in Divina where it isn't unreasonable to get a good amount of attack speed boosting auto attack damage.  Probably a solid reserve for most players, and very strong for half reserve.

:card[1539] is a very tanky Anima melee.  As usual you probably want a team buffer for your tank, but if you don't have one :card[1539] is an extremely tanky option which should make it much easier to live.  She did have some animation/behavior problems which may make it harder to ensure she is tanking for you, but if she is able to draw the enemies fire you can expect her to make it much easier to live, which may enable you to run a more aggressive backline.  I also probably missed with :card[2248] his freeze was actually extremely helpful in this event, so :card[1539]'s very high rate of paralysis should also be helpful for reducing incoming damage.  A very tanky option for newer Anima players.

:card[1538] is a weak 4* leader, probably not strong enough for anyone to spend jewels on.

:card[1523] is an easy skip, 13% more damage could be an ok stepping stone, but the melee only conditional makes it not really worth spending XP on leveling.

:card[1536] Also a fairly easy skip, not worth jewels.

:card[1530] 43% DMG reduction final wave assist is decent in some contexts, but for jewels it's a skip, look to :card[1062] which is joining auction.

:card[1537] Grab :card[2244] from the current event instead.

:card[1525] Probably too weak for most players.

:card[1535] Skip

## Exchange Card

:card[1062] is a 25% damage reduction assist.  I don't know this is a card anyone really needs, but it's a nice utility card, if you find you need more defense you can throw this on a front line card.  Worth unlocking for 2 copies.

## Rerun: New Year Spirit Battle Marathon

:card[630] has been powercrept by the new wave of crit rate carries, but for some time :card[630] was a one of the strongest self buffer due to her 50% attack speed, and 50% damage in the final wave.  She also has a damage amp.  She is still to this day a decent option for newer players who are looking for a carry in the midgame and do not have the :card[1526] pairing, but at the high end she is held back by lack of self crit, which you want on a modern carry.

:card[631] is a support melee that is geared around multi round content and skill damage.  She could be an ok option for endless or multi wave content, but you could also look to :card[1438] as a more greedy option for multi round melee for mochi.
