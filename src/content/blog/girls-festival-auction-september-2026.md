## Notable Picks

:card[1526] is a relatively strong crit rate buffer who has a hard requirement of pairing with :card[1529] who we will discuss here as well.  I think he is the strongest carry in Anima, and on paper the pairing's final round numbers are the best of the three big carry packages right now:

| Final round, one round fight | Crit rate | Crit DMG | DMG |
|---|---|---|---|
| :card[1526] + :card[1529] | 82.5% | +65% | +60% |
| :card[1721] + :card[1720] | 62.5% | +90% | — |
| :card[1401] | 52.5% | +60% | +25% |

Crit rate includes the 22.5% every ranged starts with, so :card[1526]'s 82.5% is his own 40% for having :card[1529] on the team plus her 20% Anima crit rate, and his crit damage is her 50% to the top two ATK plus her 15% all-ally wave start.  The 60% damage is his own final round ability.  That is the strongest total package of the three, and :card[1529] is doing a lot of the work — she is great in multiround content and fine in one round like World Boss where she is similar power to :card[1669] or :card[1615] but spreading her slightly smaller buffs more widely.

The catch is in the skills.  :card[1526]'s skill decreases SPD where :card[1401] and :card[1721] both amp damage, so the pairing brings no amp of its own.  A damage amp is worth about 75% while it is up and you can hold it roughly 8 seconds out of every 20, so running with no amp at all costs you around 30% damage on average, and it is worse than that in practice because the window you lose overlaps your burst.  If another card on your team is already supplying the amp during your skill windows the real gap is smaller than the direct math, you are paying a team slot instead of paying damage.  Either way it forces your hand on team building, and the cards that would fill the gap in Anima aren't as strong as the Divina or Phantasma equivalents.  Many of the best supports for Anima are not attribute locked, so they also raise :card[1401] or :card[1721] — :card[1650] and :card[514] help everyone.  Anima tends toward wide small buffs rather than the 2 target buffs that drive the meta.

So the weakness here isn't :card[1526] or the pairing, it's the support depth behind them.  That is also the thing most likely to change.  Despite all that both cards are a fairly good buy, and the pairing is strong enough for any content even if it isn't the strongest team available.  The risk is a new crit rate carry showing up.  This pair is a buy for players who want to play Anima or want to be ready if the meta shifts toward Anima, but probably a skip for most players since it may be fairly difficult or expensive to assemble both to enable the combo, and you are setting yourself up for investment in any Anima supports without knowing if it will pay off, for in the best case something slightly better than other options.

### Limited Jewel Summons

:card[1526] is the other Limited Jewel Summon this month — see Notable Picks above.

:card[1540] is a progressive crit rate card that is comparable to :card[1494].  The comparison is a bit difficult.  :card[1540] gives 10% crit rate per round, and some free HP on top of it, where :card[1494] gives 7% crit rate per round that gets 20% XP and 30% soulstones.  Both are limited to multi wave fights.  :card[1540] is the stronger card, but multi wave fights would cover Conquest and Special Story.  In Conquest :card[1540] is the winner, there's no XP, but in Special Story to most players XP is more important than the extra crit for sustain.  :card[1540]'s skill is also arguably better for new players despite its own anti-synergy with herself, Divina is awash in % Damage buffers.  In any case if you care about the XP in Special Story, :card[1540] is very expensive for a very modest gain that you won't use most of the time anyway.  If you care about min maxing or don't have :card[1494] or :card[1672] you can buy :card[1540], but if you already have :card[1494] or :card[1672] you are probably fine skipping this one.  The wider point on all of the jewel cards this month is that you are spending real money on effects you can cover without spending any, and that is most of why they land as skips rather than the cards being bad.

## Event Rewards

:card[1529] is the overall ranking reward and is covered with :card[1526] in Notable Picks above.

:card[1528] is a surprisingly usable half ranking reward.  Her skill is not great, but her abilities are competitive for Divina reserve especially in short battles.  She looks at first like she falls behind the top tier supports like :card[1578] or :card[1321], but if you run the numbers for one round content she lands close enough to them that the gap doesn't really matter.  The real separator is that their damage boosts are always on where hers waits for the final round, so :card[1578] and :card[1321] are buffing you through every wave of Conquest or Endless and she is contributing nothing until the last one.  In a one round fight the final round is the only round, which is where she catches up.  She also carries the same 25% Divina attack speed as :card[1321], so what you are really giving up is the uptime, not the size of the buff.  35% damage in the final round is competitive in short fights, and 25% attack speed is a nice bonus in Divina where it isn't unreasonable to get a good amount of attack speed boosting auto attack damage.  Probably a solid reserve for most players, and very strong for half reserve.  This one is a buy for Divina players who don't already have strong Divina supports.

:card[1539] is a very tanky Anima melee.  As usual you probably want a team buffer for your tank, but if you don't have one :card[1539] is an extremely tanky option which should make it much easier to live.  She did have some animation/behavior problems which may make it harder to ensure she is tanking for you, but if she is able to draw the enemies fire you can expect her to make it much easier to live, which may enable you to run a more aggressive backline.  I also probably missed with :card[2248] his freeze was actually extremely helpful in this event, so :card[1539]'s very high rate of paralysis should also be helpful for reducing incoming damage.  A very tanky option for newer Anima players.

:card[1538] is a weak 4\* leader, probably not strong enough for anyone to spend jewels on.  If you want a real Phantasma leader :card[734] is the one to chase, and in practice most Phantasma teams are led by :card[1650] or :card[549] anyway, both of which are Anima and lead across attributes just fine.  The bigger problem is that 13% DMG doesn't justify a team slot at all, never mind the leader slot.  We keep coming back to the same example, but :card[738] costs mochi and gives 20% DMG to Phantasma in the final wave plus 25% crit DMG to two allies every wave, which is far more out of one slot.  :card[2185] is a fairly weak healer and still a better support than this.  :card[706] as well.  There is no version of your team where 13% is the best thing a card can be doing for you.

:card[1523] is an easy skip, 13% more damage could be an ok stepping stone, but the melee only conditional makes it not really worth spending XP on leveling.  If you want that effect the assists do it better and without the restriction: :card[1305] and :card[676] are around now and should be in auction, :card[1638] is 25% to all foes in the final round and should turn up in an upcoming jewel auction, and :card[1617] should eventually land in auction as well.

:card[1536] Also a fairly easy skip, not worth jewels.

:card[1530] 43% DMG reduction final wave assist is decent in some contexts, but for jewels it's a skip, look to :card[1062] which is joining auction.

:card[1537] Grab :card[2244] from the current event instead.

:card[1525] Probably too weak for most players.

:card[1535] Skip

## Exchange Card

:card[1062] is a 25% damage reduction assist.  I don't know this is a card anyone really needs, but it's a nice utility card, if you find you need more defense you can throw this on a front line card.  Worth unlocking for 2 copies, but you probably want to sell the second copy back after sealing since you can only have 1 copy active in a team.

## Rerun: New Year Spirit Battle Marathon

:card[630] has been powercrept by the new wave of crit rate carries, but for a long time she was one of the strongest self buffers in the game with 50% attack speed, 50% damage in the final wave, and a damage amp on top.  What holds her back at the high end is the lack of self crit, which is the thing you want on a modern carry.  She was the first carry I ever bought with cash, where :card[740] was the first one I orbed, and I never really had an Anima team behind her and she was still a huge boost as a new player, so most of her power sits on the card itself.  She is also not a card I would run in any of my teams today.  Both of those are true at once, and that's the read: she will take a player who is struggling to clear and get them clearing, and she should be cheaper than assembling the :card[1526] pairing, but she is not competitive in World Boss anymore.  Buy her to solve a clearing problem, not to chase a ranking, and if you already have :card[1401], :card[1721] or :card[740] there's nothing here for you.  If you catch this before the auction goes live and the event ends, exchanging :card[1651] is the other option, also not a crit buffer but a solid midgame carry with great utility.

:card[631] is a support melee that is geared around multi round content and skill damage.  She could be an ok option for endless or multi wave content, but you could also look to :card[1438] as a more greedy option for multi round melee for mochi.  If you want tanky :card[1539] is also hitting this same auction and has less utility but more damage reduction.  She is a solid support, but you'd be paying cash for a role you can fill without it.
