## Ranking Cards

:card[1438] is the overall and is a very strong melee attacker with a strong skill. 20% damage per round is some of the highest in the game, and 25% damage reduction to highest attack is useful as a defensive. The big problem for this card is that melee cards don't make great greedy attackers due to lack of crit rate, so you will nearly always choose to stack buffs on a ranged card. Still, he is strong for a melee card, and useful in endless. But this isn't a card that will make or break your team. Price will be the thing to watch here: if he is priced around :card[1498] he is similarly useful for newer Anima players as a stepping stone. Otherwise he is relatively easy to skip for most players. **Stepping stone for newer Anima players if he lands around :card[1498] in price — strong for a melee card and useful in Endless, but easy to skip for most.**

:card[1439] was half ranking and is just weird. Life leech and 20% DR is great for a tanky card, but not really for a ranged that is standing in your back row. The main use for her is as a ranged attack bond for 7.5% for Phantasma, and there's no shortage of Phantasma tanks on auction or coming to auction. **Skip — mainly useful as a 7.5% Phantasma ranged-attack bond, and Phantasma tanks are plentiful.**

## Story Rewards

:card[1436] here is one of those tanky melee cards. 25% damage reduction is ok, self-damage buff is not ideal for a melee with low crit rate. Pretty easy skip for anyone but new players looking for a cheap Phantasma tank. :card[738] is a much better choice from auction while stock holds up for offensive team buffs, or :card[227] if you want something tankier that will also probably deal better damage. Could be ok as a stepping stone if it's cheap for new players, otherwise an easy skip. **Easy skip except new players wanting a cheap Phantasma tank; :card[738] or :card[227] are better auction picks.**

:card[1435] — Mr Xmas himself — is a 3-card progressive skill damage card. :card[1402] coming next month is probably more expensive but has better defensive value, and :card[1576] in October is roughly the same with additional DR. There is really no shortage of progressive multi-target casters; these can be ok for multiwave content or endless, and if this is something you want you probably go for :card[1402] for endless, otherwise just buy the cheapest available. Could be worth a look for newer players if the price is right, but he's a stepping stone to better cards in the same niche, or cards that are better overall. **Skip for most — a stepping stone; grab the cheapest progressive caster, or :card[1402] next month for endless.**

:card[1431] is worth a quick mention for PvP. PvP farming is where you throw the match on purpose, so a weak defender is exactly what you want — her low stats and a skill that doesn't interfere with the attacking team make her a fine pick for an awful defense at level 80, depending on which farming you're doing. Nothing for PvE. **Niche — a throwaway PvP farming defense that works at 80; skip for PvE.**

## Limited Jewel Summons

:card[1437] is an excellent Phantasma defensive assist. His two abilities also work multiplicatively with one another, so an MLB copy reduces enemy attack frequency by ~33% (a 50% ATK speed slow works out to ~33% fewer attacks, not 50%) and damage taken by 15%, which results in an overall ~43% reduction in incoming damage — massive. In addition the abilities stack, although this was verified by the devs as not intended, so it could be fixed. Stacking behavior on the DR is confirmed but doesn't seem to work additively for the same ability, so I'm not going to give concrete numbers; it would require more clear testing, but it is confirmed to work. I haven't tested directly, but it's likely a single copy can allow you to 1-heal world boss for a Phantasma team, and it's great in Phantasma endless teams — although it's unlikely to be enough to make it so you don't still need :card[2373]. It actually synergizes extremely hard with her, making Phantasma clearing very easy and team building very flexible. That being said, excellent defensive assists are not something you need to complete any content to date; you can generally just power through it. But if there's one pick for best card of the auction, it's clearly this card. Very strong, but expensive, defensive niche card — not required, but worth picking up if you play Phantasma and you have the jewels for it. **Best card of the auction — an excellent (but expensive) Phantasma defensive assist that synergizes hard with :card[2373]; not required for any content, but a strong pickup for Phantasma players.**

:card[1992] is an ok Phantasma healer. 30% DMG to Phantasma is fairly good when compared to options for DMG from healers in general; in practice for Phantasma passive DMG :card[2373] is the better gacha choice. Her skill is good for newer players, but with the addition of :card[1401] is generally overkill and/or hard to use. She is hard to recommend in the same way :card[1735] was recently. Crit buffers for :card[1401] teams are often throwing away most of a skill, so while on paper these skills look good, they require manipulating ATK values to get value out of them. In either case these are decent healers, but most Phantasma players should have at least :card[1672] from recent events, and newer Phantasma players can probably save jewels and go with :card[655] or :card[526], although stock of these is waning. :card[1992] is a fine Phantasma healer, but fine isn't good enough to spend jewels on for most players. **Skip — a fine Phantasma healer, but :card[2373] is the better gacha pick and most players already have better (:card[1672], or :card[655]/:card[526] for newer players); fine isn't worth jewels.**

## Rerun — Maze of Red Leaves

:card[596] gives 60% skill damage and a 20% chance to stun on normal attack. I imagine he could be very strong in real PvP, but very few people do that. Maybe there's some stun niche to try to attack-speed up and stun-lock something, but in most cases you are better off just blasting through. Skip. **Skip — a PvP/stun niche only.**

:card[597] is a Ranged attacker whose ability slows all foes' ATK speed by 45% at the start of every wave, with no limitations on it. A 45% slow works out to ~31% fewer enemy attacks, so that's a real cut to the incoming normal-attack damage over a fight. Between that unrestricted slow and her skill she could be fairly strong for endless, but she's one-dimensional — you'd really want a second team buff or a self-buffing ability on top, which newer cards in the same vein bring. :card[690] wins in virtually every context: she's easier to slot and stacks multiple team buffs (a per-wave Anima crit-damage boost, plus a 30% slow and damage amp on the final wave). Her only real blind spot is endless — it runs a fixed 100 rounds, so a final-wave trigger effectively never lands there, and that narrow niche is the one place 597's every-wave slow pulls ahead. The catch is timing: 690 last ran around June 2025 and isn't back until her rerun, which the ~21-month auction queue puts around early 2027, so if you want the slow now and don't have her, 597 is a passable stopgap. :card[1402] softens that too — it comes to auction next month without costing jewels, trading all-foe coverage for a single-target 50% slow while ramping its own skill damage each wave. **Situational — :card[690] is the better card almost everywhere but won't rerun until ~early 2027; 597 is a passable stopgap for an unrestricted slow now, and only truly beats 690 in endless. :card[1402] next month is a partial fill without jewels.**

## Exchange

:card[1313] is the exchange card and is an assist card that restores 25% HP to all allies at the start of each wave. This ability is in general not very strong, so it's not worth giving up an assist slot for it. Might just be one for the collectors. **Skip — a weak wave-start heal not worth an assist slot; collectors only.**
