Well a bit of a suprise with this one, following up the huge :card[1401] and :card[1444] auction with the divina competitor :card[1721] and early at that.   It's a good suprise for some, but other might be dry, in which case don't worry too much :card[1721] will be returning again in January.

## Notable Picks

:card[1721] is a massive Divina carry with 40% Crit Rate and 40% Crit DMG to self at the start of the final wave.

I went through the :card[1401] side of this last month so I won't re-run it — short fights are his, long fights are hers — but I did write "neither is buyable right now anyway" and four days later here he is, so his side deserves a proper go.  The part I undersold is the crit rate.  It's the stat you can't cheaply buy, and where she brings 30% he brings 40%, just paid out in one lump on the final wave instead of from wave one, that makes it 10% easier to chase crit cap to ensure your skills land for full effect.  He also doesn't silence himself, where she silences the whole Phantasma team including herself, so on the wave where both are switched on his skill is firing and hers isn't for 5s.  The Orihime pairing is worth less than it looks: the +10 levels work off *any* Orihime-tagged card and come out to about 10% more ATK, and while :card[510] covers that from the permanent auction for pocket change, :card[1720] is the partner you'd actually want, less for the levels than for her skill, which has replaced :card[739] in a lot of teams and is a big part of how teams reach the skill damage cap.  Both of these cards can stand alone, but are just a little better together.

Outside a world boss he's far less grim than "dead weight in a five wave fight," because Divina buys crit rate by the wave anyway — :card[1540] at 10% to all Divina every wave, :card[1494] at 7% on top of a skill that hands it to the whole team, and :card[1672] at 10% to your top two, who is Phantasma but has neither ability attribute locked.  Those cover the early waves, he covers the last one, and the last one is typically the longest.  :card[1311], :card[553] and :card[616] are worth a look if multiround is all you do, but treat them as alternatives rather than upgrades.  On the money, since this one is cash: what you're buying is the final found crit rate.  None of these alternatives have crit rate, and crit rate is expensive enough to print that he won't be quietly powercrept.  None of it is urgent though — he's back in January with :card[1720] beside him, and that's arguably the better buy since you get the pair in one go.  But given the event was recent you may already have :card[1720] in which case you can get to building your Divina team early if you want.

**Top pick for Divina and a safe buy — 40% crit rate is the one thing Divina can't get elsewhere. Best in single round content and perfectly good everywhere else. Buy now if you do world boss, wait for January with :card[1720] if you don't.**

:card[1734] is a card where last month I filed this one under "worth knowing about less as an option than as a sign of where the pressure comes from," and buyability aside that mostly still holds.  It isn't a :card[1444] competitor and you shouldn't be choosing between them.  One copy — and one is all it does anything for — buys 40% skill DMG and 40% crit DMG on whoever wears it, plus 35% DMG to every Phantasma on the team, including the :card[1401] already wearing your :card[1444].  The simplest way to read it is a solid team assist with free self damage stapled on.  That team half stands up next to anything you'd otherwise put in the slot: :card[664] is the staple %DMG assist at the same 35% but stops at your top two, :card[1310] is the closest match with the same 35% to Phantasma on the final wave, trading 15% team max HP for the 40/40 self here, which is a good trade in an offensive slot, and :card[673] isn't strictly behind either since its 30% comes with 25% skill DMG to Phantasma running all battle.  What none of them carry is the silence, though on a :card[1401] team she silences the whole Phantasma team on the final wave anyway, so that bill is already paid.  On a Phantasma team without her, you're adding a silence that wasn't there.

Where it might do more than that is a team already spilling part of :card[1444]'s 75% against the 999,999 skill cap.  Skill damage you can't spend is worth nothing, and the 35% is DMG dealt rather than skill DMG, so it hits autos and keeps counting past the cap — and if you can find a helper :card[1401] wearing one too, you get both instances at once.  The catch is that the overcap is the hard part.  Andromeda teams reach the skill cap far less easily than Hikoboshi teams do, so the condition that justifies all this is rarest on the attribute that wants it.  Past that it's a three carry card in a two carry game, its 35% hitting every Phantasma where nearly every team buff stops at your top two, and that future hasn't arrived yet.

**Not required, worth considering — a team assist comparable to :card[664], :card[1310] and :card[673] with free self damage on top, plus some upside on skill capped teams and if a third carry ever becomes a play. If you bought :card[1444] last month you're in good shape.**

## Event Cards

I covered most of the rest of this batch when the event ran and nothing has changed since, so these are quick restatements.  The one thing that *has* changed is access — several of these were standard gacha or LJS at release and are now sitting here where you can just buy the copy you want.

:card[1730] is an ATK-heavy melee with a 2-target skill and self progressive ATK.  Stats are high, but she still struggles to find a niche.  **Skip — high stats, no clear niche.**

:card[1731] is the interesting one.  Her progressive 15% DMG to Phantasma is bigger than :card[740] and great for endless, but it only unlocks at 4 copies, which is why I said don't pull when she was standard gacha.  Buying copies directly changes that maths a bit — you can actually finish her now rather than waiting on the pity of the pull, though four copies at auction prices is its own kind of expensive.  **Strong long term, awkward to get there — the 15% Phantasma DMG beats :card[740] in endless but needs 4 copies. Worth it only if you're committed to Phantasma endless; otherwise keep accumulating slowly.**

:card[1735] is still a strong Phantasma single-target buffer at 71% crit rate and 100% crit damage with progressive skill damage, and my view hasn't moved: the niche is shrinking because that crit rate overcaps on a carry who already has crit buffs, which is most carries now.  I said much the same about :card[1992] in the O Holy Night writeup.  **Skip — a good buffer for a niche that's closing; :card[655] does close enough for mochi.**

:card[1736] is a 3-target wave-clearer with some play as a greedy endless attacker, but skill damage and attack speed are isolated buffs that don't multiply off one another.  I said a copy was fine if you were pulling anyway back when she was an LJS card, which isn't the same as paying for her on purpose.  **Don't buy — awkward to invest in, and there's nothing here you need.**

:card[513] is back again after the February rerun and hasn't improved.  He wants :card[510] for full value and she's long since been powercrept for Divina, so the pair doesn't amount to much.  Note he's cost 4, which is a real tax on a card doing this little, and as covered above he does **not** trigger :card[1721]'s level bonus.  **Collectors only.**

## 4-Star Assists

:card[1723] is 55% skill damage on the final wave and nothing else.  That's a dead end in two directions: for a support slot you want an assist that gives *team* buffs, and for a greedy attacker you'd rather save toward a multi-buff assist like :card[1444] or :card[707] that does the work of several of these at once.  I've called cards like this a stepping stone before, but that advice was for mochi — nobody should be spending real money to bridge a gap that auction fills cheaply.  **Don't buy — a single-buff assist isn't worth cash; save toward :card[1444] or :card[707].**

:card[1732] is a final-wave damage amp and the same story.  It's a fine little card if it ever turns up for mochi, but there's no version of this that justifies jewels.  **Don't buy.**
