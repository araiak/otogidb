This one surprised me but it's nice to see some standard gacha cards make an appearance especially :card[2373].  It's a short one, the auction runs until September 28th.  Anyway let's get into it.

---

## Notable Picks

:card[2373] is a top Phantasma healer pick, and a mandatory pick for Phantasma endless teams chasing the 100 floor leaderboard.  She brings 45% damage boost to the two highest Phantasma attackers on your team, which is excellent at only 2 copies.  She also gives 4% max HP per wave to Phantasma at MLB, which is nice to have but mostly for endless where it is pretty much mandatory if you want to finish 100 floors.  That isn't a critical niche, you can clear much faster splitting it into 2 50 waves for the cost of 1 extra SP, which is a great trade, but if you want to add your name to the leaderboard you can borrow her, or now's your chance to avoid orbing or needing to rng into 5 copies.  I mentioned :card[2373] is a top healer, and this may sometimes be overshadowed by :card[549] who was slightly better in my opinion, or :card[1650] who takes your 45% from 2 to 5 allies, and adds 20 levels on top which significantly overshadows all healers.  Both of those also buff every attribute where :card[2373] only reaches Phantasma.  But those cost your leader slot so if you wanted to run :card[734] or you don't have either of those, :card[2373] is probably the best option available for shorter battles.  In longer battles it's easy to make a case for :card[1672].  Outside of those 3 healers :card[2373] is pretty hard to beat, :card[1992] is less damage to more cards, but that isn't necessarily a good trade, :card[1735] and :card[752] are both excellent single target buffers, but I think more teams are leaning into multiple carries which makes :card[2373] a better choice.  :card[2373] is strong, and viable at only 2 copies, she might not be especially cheap, but that speaks to her power.  She's a good pick for moderate or higher Phantasma spenders who want her for endless or don't have any of the listed cards.

**Buy for Phantasma, especially endless** — 45% DMG to your top two Phantasma is excellent at 2 copies and the best non-leader healer for shorter fights, but :card[1650] and :card[549] outclass her if you're happy to give up the leader slot.

## Other 5-Star Cards

:card[2374] is a less clear choice, she offers a 2 target damage skill with a guaranteed stun on the two enemies with the highest ATK.  Stunning the two biggest hitters has some defensive value, but 2 targets is a weird place for a damage skill as there isn't really any place that has 2 big targets so you tend to want wave clear or single target damage skills.  To be good she would need to make up for it with great abilities, and her other ability is good, but it doesn't do enough on its own.  Her 35% final wave damage amp is only 5% more than :card[661] or :card[690], and both of those bring more with them for close to the same amp: :card[661] also cuts the damage of the enemy with the highest ATK by 30% every wave, and :card[690]'s 30% hits all foes, slows their attack speed by 30%, and comes with 25% crit DMG for two Anima allies.  That 5% might have been meaningful if not for the release of :card[1631] that brings 35% damage reduction and 35% damage amp every wave rather than only the final wave, plus a small crit damage boost and a great skill.  :card[1631] is the better pick in every way.

**Skip** — a 2 target stun skill with no clear niche, and her 35% final wave amp is beaten by :card[1631]'s 35% every wave with damage reduction on top.
