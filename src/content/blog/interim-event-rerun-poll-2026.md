The devs have put up a [poll](https://wj.qq.com/s2/28029092/85o9) for an interim event before Halloween starts. It's a cool opportunity to replay an older event and bring back some cards. I just wanted to quickly draw attention to a few cards and events that might be of interest to players. I may have missed some events, but these are the ones that caught my eye.

---

## A New Year's Tale of Laziness

:card[209] is old enough that it's pretty rare to see in auction and is still pretty decent. In general I think it's been powercrept, but it can still find use in world boss teams, especially outside of Divina, who has :card[1578].

---

## Once Upon a Root...

This one includes :card[492] and :card[495]. :card[492] is a fairly rare and expensive healer/tank who brings 55% skill damage to the top 2 Divina ATK. There aren't a lot of options with this ability or anything similar in Divina, so she can be nice for flexibility when building, and she can allow :card[1721] teams to pivot more abilities toward attack damage while still ensuring skills crit. :card[495] has mostly been replaced by :card[1672], :card[1784], :card[1494] or :card[1540].

---

## Alice in the Serious Kingdom

This brings :card[642] and :card[644]. :card[642] has been fairly powercrept, but :card[644] has in some ways actually gotten better with the releases of :card[1488] and :card[1584]. It feels like there are more healer XP boosters than you have slots for, but ranged attackers are fairly limited, with the strongest being :card[1494] or :card[570]. A high attacker with an ATK skill like :card[644] would probably be a nice add for a lot of players looking to increase the strength of a full-on XP farming team.

---

## To the Source of the Milky Way

This includes the legendary :card[514], which is still a staple in a lot of players' world boss teams and cost a ton on its re-re-run. A re-run of this event would surely be a good opportunity for a lot of players.

---

## Otogi 2nd Anniversary Special Episode: Incident of the Surprise Assault at the Sakuma Mansion

Probably my personal top pick. We just had :card[1650], but Anima ranged carries are few and far between, and an uptick in supply for :card[543] would probably make Anima a bit more common. Even if she isn't a top tier carry anymore, she could make a pretty solid support for :card[1526] teams if you can find space for her passive and active damage amps — that's exactly what :card[1526] really lacks.

For this and the next one, :card[550] is still a staple, and :card[553] is still a decent support option even years later.

---

## Otogi 2nd Anniversary Special Episode: The Plan for the Crusade Against Tosho Daigongen

This brings :card[549], which could be great for players who missed out on :card[1650]. It would make :card[549] a lot more accessible, which is generally good for the game, but it probably doesn't offer much to players who got :card[1650] in the anniversary event.

---

## Record of the Spirit Halloween

This brings :card[580], the XP buffer. He's common to find as a helper, but you can use your own copy and a helper's, so if you want to maximize XP boosts he's a card you want to have. He just had a re-re-run, but prices have gone up as stock has dwindled over time.

---

## Clavicle of the King

Probably another banger choice, though now that we're past the 4th anniversary many of the cards have been powercrept.

- :card[734] is still a great Phantasma lead option that we've talked about many times.
- :card[738] is a staple for F2P Phantasma players, but supply is dwindling, and a re-run would surely fix that.
- :card[740] is still strong despite being powercrept by :card[1651].
- :card[739] is a very solid damage buffer that was a staple for a long time. She's been surpassed by :card[1720] but can still hold her own.

---

## Go Vote

There are a lot of events here I can get excited for, so please [vote](https://wj.qq.com/s2/28029092/85o9)! I kind of hope this is something we might see again between events — not too often, but every now and then it would be a nice opportunity to restock cards and make newer players feel included rather than locked out of critical event cards. :card[514] and :card[549] come to mind.
